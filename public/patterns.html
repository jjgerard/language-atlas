What the entries have in common

The maps answer what one place does. This page answers what the places have in common — and, just as often, what none of them record. Everything here is counted from the entries as they stand, so it changes when they do.

Ask it something. Pick two things the atlas records and it counts them against each other across all four maps at once — or describe what you want and let it choose the two. The findings, the timeline and the coverage tables are below.

Nothing here is read out of entry text — the wording is deliberately hedged, and a classifier over it fails hardest where the answer is interesting. A question the atlas cannot answer is told so rather than answered with the nearest thing that fits.

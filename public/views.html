One field, every country

A map answers one question about one place. This page turns that ninety degrees: pick a question and read every country's answer to it in one column, so the answers can be compared rather than visited one at a time.

A blank is not a nil. Five things can be in a cell: an answer; no such rule here, established from the instruments; looked, found nothing; not applicable; and blank, meaning only that nobody has looked. Reading a blank as a zero measures attention, not policy.

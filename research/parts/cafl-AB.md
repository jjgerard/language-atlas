### CA-AB|Alberta
STATUS: documented

HEADLINE FINDING: **Alberta has no compulsory second language at any grade.**
Second languages sit in an optional time block in Grades 1–9 and are one of
several ways to fill a 10-credit elective block for the Alberta High School
Diploma. Alberta's own terms are "French as a second language (FSL)",
"international languages" and "First Nations, Métis and Inuit languages" — not
"foreign languages".

NEGATIVES / CORRECTION — READ THIS BEFORE ADDING A POLICY ROW:
 - The commonly repeated "Alberta 2009 second-language policy reversal" DID NOT
   CHECK OUT. I could not retrieve any Alberta government announcement, news
   release or reliable account of a provincial mandatory Grades 4–9 second
   language being introduced and then cancelled in 2009. What I could verify is a
   **Calgary Board of Education** decision: the board introduced a Grade 4 second
   language requirement in **2006**, extended it a grade a year, and dropped it in
   **April 2011**. That is a single school board, not the province, and the source
   is CBC, a secondary source. Do NOT convert this into a provincial policyHistory
   row. Note also the CBC story carries its own published correction: "In fact
   French was not mandatory for those grades; a second language course was
   mandatory, and that requirement is being dropped."
 - Term count proving the negative on the statute: in the retrieved Education Act
   office consolidation (ab_education_act_e00p3.txt, 603,561 chars, current as of
   11 December 2025) the phrase "second language" occurs **0** times. Sanity check
   on words that ARE present in the same file: "French" 3, "language of
   instruction" 3, "Cree" 1.

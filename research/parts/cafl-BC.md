### CA-BC|British Columbia
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: BC's requirement is a generic "second language", not French
specifically — Core French is only the default. A local Indigenous language can
satisfy it. Nothing in BC calls these "foreign languages".

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Second language compulsory Grades 5 to 8 only, under Ministerial Order M295/95 s 4
     - Requirement is for "a language other than English", not French specifically
     - Core French is the default only where the board offers no alternative
     - Exempt: students with an IEP, ELL students, and Grade 6 late French immersion
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Requirement stops at Grade 8; Grade 9 upward is elective
     - A local Aboriginal language course can substitute under M295/95 s 6
     - That route needs a board agreement with a band council or treaty first nation
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - No second language in the Graduation Program Order Table 1 required areas of learning
     - Required areas: Language Arts, Social Studies, Science, Maths, PHE, Arts/ADST, Career
     - French immersion diploma adds FLS-immersion 10, 11, 12 plus 12 credits of French courses
     - Programme francophone adds Français langue première and Français langue et culture 12
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - K-9 guides: Core French, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, German, Italian, Punjabi
     - Grade 10-12 courses exist for each of those, plus Introductory 11 entry courses
     - Template packages add Arabic, Croatian, Farsi, Russian
     - Boards may develop local language courses under the Board Authorized Course Order
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - No prescribed minutes in the order; it sets learning standards, not time
     - Policy asks boards to keep the same language across Grades 5 to 8 for continuity
 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - No provincial second-language exam in the English-language stream
     - French immersion graduates sit the Évaluation de littératie de la 12e année - FLS immersion
     - Programme francophone has its own Français langue première literacy assessments
 - field: teacherSupply
   bullets:
     - Not documented from a BC government source this session
 - field: higherEducation
   bullets:
     - Not documented from a BC government source this session
 - field: uptake
   bullets:
     - No BC enrolment figure retrieved from an official source this session
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - Policy: all students should have the chance to learn an Indigenous language
     - Around 20 First Nations languages have provincial 5-12 Integrated Resource Packages
     - Only those with a Grades 5-8 provincial curriculum count for the second-language requirement
     - Graduation now needs 4 credits of Indigenous-focused coursework (Sched. 1, M302/04)
 - field: policyHistory
   bullets:
     - 1995 M295/95 sets required areas of learning; second language in Grades 5-8
     - 1997 Language Education Policy issued, revised 2004
     - 2016 M307/16 rewrites the order for the redesigned K-9 curriculum
     - 2023 M183/23 adds the 4-credit Indigenous-focused graduation requirement

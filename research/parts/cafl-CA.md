### CA|Canada (national)
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: there is no national curriculum and no federal second-language
requirement. What is federal is (a) the constitutional minority-language
education right, (b) the Minister of Canadian Heritage's mandate under the
Official Languages Act to help people learn both official languages, and (c) the
CMEC–Canada Protocol and the transfer money that flows under it. This entry
describes those, not a summary of the provinces.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - No national curriculum and no federal second-language requirement
     - CMEC-Canada Protocol cl 1.1: education is exclusive provincial and territorial jurisdiction
     - Federal role is funding and the Charter s 23 minority-language education right
     - "Second language" federally means the other official language, English or French
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Set province by province, not federally
     - Protocol funds second-language instruction but does not prescribe grades or hours
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Federal instruments cover English and French only
     - Protocol defines minority and second language as "the two official languages of Canada"
     - Other languages fall outside the federal transfer entirely
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Federal transfer, not instructional time: max $235,520,472 a year under the 2019-23 Protocol
     - Of that, $86,392,115 a year is the second-language envelope
     - $149,128,357 a year is the minority-language envelope
     - Plus $15,000,000 a year extra for minority-language education (cl 4.3)
 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - No federal assessment of second-language attainment
     - P/Ts file annual and periodic reports to Canadian Heritage under the bilateral agreements
 - field: teacherSupply
   bullets:
     - "Support for educational staff" is one of six funded areas of intervention in the Protocol
     - Delivery and teacher certification remain provincial
 - field: higherEducation
   bullets:
     - OLA s 43(1)(e): Canadian Heritage may help P/Ts create chances to learn both languages
     - CCMEC administers the Explore, Destination Clic and Odyssey bursary and monitor programs
     - These run under separate contribution agreements, not the Protocol budget
 - field: uptake
   bullets:
     - No pan-Canadian enrolment figure retrieved this session (see negatives)
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - Federal Protocol covers only official-language minorities, English and French
     - Indigenous languages sit outside it, under separate instruments
 - field: policyHistory
   bullets:
     - 1982 Charter s 23 creates the minority-language education right where numbers warrant
     - 1988 Official Languages Act gives Canadian Heritage the second-language mandate
     - 2019-2023 Protocol sets the $235.5m/yr transfer; Quebec does not subscribe to it
     - 2023 Bill C-13 (S.C. 2023 c.15 s.22) amends OLA s.43

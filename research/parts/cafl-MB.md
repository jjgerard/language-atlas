### CA-MB|Manitoba

STATUS: documented

FRAMING NOTE: In Manitoba French is one of Canada's two official languages and the
department's own categories are "Français Program" (French first language),
"French Immersion Program" and "French in the English Program" (formerly Basic
French). Other languages are departmentally called "International and Heritage
Languages" (grant name: "Indigenous and International Languages"). Indigenous
languages are recorded under regionalMinorityLanguages.

NEGATIVES:

 - The Education Administration Miscellaneous Provisions Regulation, M.R. 468/88 R
   (https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/468-88r.php, http 200; PDF
   https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/regs/current/_pdf-regs.php?reg=468/88%20r, http 200)
   was retrieved and read in full (899 lines of extracted PDF text). It contains NO
   required-subjects list, NO time allocations and NO French provision. Term count
   over the extracted text: "French" 0 (the only hit in the HTML version is the
   site's language-toggle link), "language" 0, "instruction" 10 — all in the sense
   of "instruction by correspondence", "grade 12 instruction", "classroom
   instruction". Sanity-check terms that ARE present: "principal" and "school
   board" appear throughout. This regulation is NOT the instrument that governs
   Manitoba's language requirements.

 - THERE IS NO COMPULSORY SECOND LANGUAGE IN MANITOBA AT ANY GRADE. This is
   stated twice in the department's own 2026 guidelines: "French is an optional
   subject area in the English Program" and "French courses are optional at all
   grade levels". The guidelines instead ask divisions "Does your school division
   mandate French courses? If so, at what grade levels?" — confirming the decision
   sits with the division, not the province.

 - No language credit is compulsory for a Manitoba high school diploma in the
   English Program. Term count over the extracted text of the official 17+13 credit
   chart (grad_req_en.pdf): "compulsory" 7, "French" 1, "other languages" 2 — and
   the single "French" occurrence and both "other languages" occurrences fall
   inside the OPTIONAL CREDITS list. No occurrence of "French" appears in the
   compulsory column.

 - Manitoba's Public Schools Act s.79 imposes no duty to teach a second language.
   Its only compulsory-subject rule, s.79(6), requires ENGLISH as a subject in
   Grades IV–XII where French is the language of instruction. Read in full.

 - The Aboriginal Languages Recognition Act, C.C.S.M. c. A1.5 was read in full (it
   is three sections long). It contains no education, curriculum or schooling
   provision at all — only a preamble, a recognition clause, a C.C.S.M. reference
   and a coming-into-force clause. Manitoba Education's own Q&A confirms this: it
   "has not resulted in changes to the Public School Act in terms of instruction or
   to funding of Indigenous languages."

 - higherEducation: NOT DOCUMENTED. Nothing on post-secondary language study or
   university language entrance requirements was retrieved for Manitoba in this
   session. The only post-secondary item verified is the French Immersion for
   Teachers programme at the Université de Saint-Boniface, recorded under
   teacherSupply. Leave higherEducation empty rather than infer.

 - Contradiction recorded, not resolved: Manitoba Education's bilingual Q&A page
   says bilingual programming is offered for "Cree, German, Hebrew, and Ukrainian";

# fl — Foreign languages in school | Canada: Newfoundland and Labrador

### CA-NL|Newfoundland and Labrador
STATUS: documented

FRAMING NOTE: In NL the second language is FRENCH, an official language of Canada.
The provincial sources call the programs "Core French", "Intensive Core French",
"expanded core French" and "French immersion", grouped as "French as a Second
Language" — never "foreign language". `Français langue première` is a separate
minority-language (Charter s.23) program, not FSL. Indigenous languages
(Inuttitut, Innu-aimun, Mi'kmaw) are recorded under regionalMinorityLanguages.

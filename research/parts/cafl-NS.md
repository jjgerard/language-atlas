### CA-NS|Nova Scotia
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: Nova Scotia calls it "French Second Language" (FSL) and the compulsory
course "Core French" / "Français de base". Not a foreign language. Nova Scotia
also has a Gaelic Studies course, which is a genuine regional-heritage language
entry, not a foreign-language one.

The provincial curriculum index lists exactly three programme streams — English
Programs, French Second Language, French First Language. No international /
modern-languages curriculum stream (Spanish, German, Mandarin, Latin) appears on
it. That is evidence about the PROVINCIAL CURRICULUM INDEX, not proof that no
school offers such a course: Nova Scotia recognises "Locally developed courses
approved by the department" for credit (Credits for Graduation Diploma PDF), so
modern-language courses could exist at board level. State it that way, not as
"Nova Scotia offers no other languages".

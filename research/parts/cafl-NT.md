### CA-NT|Northwest Territories

STATUS: documented

FRAMING NOTE (for the entry author, not a bullet). The NWT has eleven official
languages, nine of them Indigenous. Indigenous languages there are official
languages of the territory and languages of instruction, not "foreign languages"
and not a "regional/minority" provision — and the Education Act's compulsory
second-language duty (s.73(3)) is a duty to teach *an Official Language*, so the
thing the NWT actually mandates is not a foreign language at all. French is
separately the s.23 Charter minority language and is handled by a distinct set of
instruments. The `regionalMinorityLanguages` field is the wrong frame for the
Indigenous languages and the entry's first bullet should say so.

One correction to the task brief: "Our Languages" did NOT succeed Dene Kede and
Inuuqatigiit. All three are current. Dene Kede and Inuuqatigiit are the
"foundational curricula" (worldview / culture, all subjects); Our Languages (2020)
is the JK-12 Indigenous *language* curriculum that sits alongside them. Both the
2018 ILE Policy and the January 2026 Graduation Program Implementation Guide list
them as concurrently in force.

NEGATIVES (read the sources, found the absence):

 1. NO COMPULSORY FOREIGN LANGUAGE, AND NO COMPULSORY "SECOND LANGUAGE" IN THAT
    SENSE. Term counts on the retrieved Education Act text
    (nt_edact.txt, pdftotext -layout -enc UTF-8 of education.a.pdf):
        "second language"        0 occurrences
        "foreign language"       0 occurrences
        "immersion"              0 occurrences
        "Indigenous"             0 occurrences
    sanity-check counts on the SAME file, words that ARE present:
        "language of instruction"  18 occurrences
        "Official Language"         9 occurrences
        "Aboriginal"               11 occurrences
    The Act's compulsory-language rule is s.73(3) and it is expressed entirely in
    terms of Official Languages. The Act uses "aboriginal", never "Indigenous".

 2. NO LANGUAGE CREDIT IS REQUIRED FOR THE NWT HIGH SCHOOL DIPLOMA. Two
    independent official statements: the ECE Graduation Requirements page lists
    every required subject area and contains none; and the January 2026 NWT
    Graduation Program Implementation Guide states in its subject table that
    Second Language has "No required credits".

 3. NO STATUTORY OR REGULATORY MINIMUM INSTRUCTIONAL TIME for Indigenous
    language instruction was found. The ILE Policy says "dedicated time" and does
    not quantify it; the Our Languages Curriculum discusses program models but
    sets no minutes. The only quantified figure I found anywhere is the Core
    French one ("at least 30 minutes a day"), and that is French, on an ECE web
    page, not in an instrument.

 4. INUKTITUT IS AN OFFICIAL LANGUAGE BUT HAS NO GRADE 10-12 COURSE. The word
    "Inuktitut" occurs 0 times in the whole NWT Graduation Program Implementation
    Guide. The nine Indigenous OLC course streams in Appendix A are Dene Yatie,
    Dene Zhatıé, Dinjii Zhuʼ Ginjik, Dëne Sųłiné Yatıé, Inuinnaqtun,
    Inuvialuktun, Dene Kǝdǝ́, Tłı̨chǫ and nēhiyawēwin. Do not write "courses in
    all nine official Indigenous languages".

 5. "OUR LANGUAGES" DID NOT REPLACE DENE KEDE / INUUQATIGIIT (correcting the
    brief's premise). Both the 2018 ILE Policy and the January 2026 graduation
    guide treat all three as current, and the ECE JK-12 School Curriculum page
    still lists "Dene Kede and Inuuqatigiit" as a live curriculum area. The
    relationship is stated on the OLC page: "The curriculum is aligned with the
    two foundational curricula, Dene Kede and Inuuqatigiit".

 6. FRENCH IMMERSION ENROLMENT: no NWT figure verified. Statistics Canada table
    37-10-0009 was not usable (its CSV zip endpoint returns an HTML interstitial),
    and cpf.ca refuses connections from this host. Neither the ECE Annual Report
    on Official Languages 2023-24 nor the graduation guide gives a French
    immersion or Core French enrolment count. Leave a French uptake number OUT.

 7. NO "JUNIOR SECONDARY / SENIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL HANDBOOK" is current. ECE site
    search for "senior secondary school handbook" returned no such document; the
    live instrument is the NWT Graduation Program Implementation Guide, Grades 10
    to 12, updated January 2026. The ECE Ministerial Directives index likewise
    lists no language directive — the most recent language-relevant one, the 2004
    ALCBE Directive, is absent from that index because it was replaced by the 2018
    ILE Policy.

 8. THE OFFICIAL LANGUAGES ACT ITSELF SAYS ALMOST NOTHING ABOUT SCHOOLS. The word
    "education" occurs twice in the whole consolidated Act, both in s.26(2)(c)
    (the Minister's duty to promote Official Languages education in schools,
    post-secondary institutions and adult literacy programs). The Act carries no
    language-of-instruction provision; that is entirely the Education Act's job.

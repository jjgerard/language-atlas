### CA-NU|Nunavut
STATUS: documented

FRAMING — READ FIRST: Inuktut is NOT a foreign language and NOT minority
provision in Nunavut. It is an official language and a medium of instruction, and
statute obliges the territory to produce secondary graduates "fully proficient in
Inuktut". The interesting second-language questions in Nunavut are the reverse
ones: English as an additional language, and Inuktut taught as a second language
in the francophone school. The Act that carries all this was RENAMED in September
2025 from *Inuit Language Protection Act* to *Inuktut Protection Act*.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Not a foreign-language system: Inuktut is official and a medium of instruction
     - Inuktut Protection Act s 8(1): every parent has the right to Inuktut instruction for their child
     - That right has been in force for K-3 since 1 July 2009
     - Extended to all other primary and secondary grades on 11 November 2020
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - District Education Authorities choose a bilingual education model for their schools
     - Since 2020 they may only pick a model their community's teaching capacity can deliver
     - The Minister approves the model and may direct which applies by grade and programme
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Statutory goal: secondary graduates "fully proficient in Inuktut", spoken and written
     - Grade 12 Inuktut-as-first-language obligation does not bite until 1 July 2039
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Inuktut covers Inuktitut and Inuinnaqtun, with separate implementation timetables
     - Inuinnaqtun provisions apply only in the designated western area
     - French is provided by the Commission scolaire francophone du Nunavut
     - CSFN must also teach Inuktut as a second language
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Education Act Schedule sets application dates, not hours
     - Inuktitut first language: Grade 4 in 2026 rising to Grade 12 in 2039
     - Inuktitut second language: Grade 4 in 2028 rising to Grade 12 in 2034
     - Inuinnaqtun: Grade 4 in 2030 rising to Grade 12 in 2037
 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - Act requires Inuktut competency targets and measures of assessment
     - Records must be kept of individual attainment and programme outcomes
 - field: teacherSupply
   bullets:
     - Teaching capacity is the binding constraint, named as such in the 2020 reform
     - Minister must maintain an Inuktut teacher retention and recruitment strategy
     - Minister must assist Nunavut Arctic College's Inuktut teacher education programmes
     - Federal second-language transfer to Nunavut: $649,746 a year, 2019-23 Protocol
 - field: higherEducation
   bullets:

 - field: uptake

NEGATIVES / CORRECTIONS:
 - The 2019 deadline in the 2008 Education Act was MISSED, not met. The Government
   of Nunavut says so in its own words (quoted above), and Bill 25 replaced it.
 - The Act's NAME has changed. In the current official consolidation
   (nu_ilpa_official.txt, consolidation date 18 September 2025) the string "Inuit
   Language" occurs 0 times and "Inuktut" occurs 203 times; the amending statute
   credited is S.Nu. 2025, c.22, s.1-23 etc., in force 18 September 2025. The
   Education Act Schedule retrieved (consolidation current to 1 July 2021) still
   uses "Inuit Language Arts" — so the two consolidations are at different vintages
   and the older wording is quoted where that is the text on the page.
 - Nunavut does not report French immersion enrolment to Statistics Canada, and no
   Nunavut enrolment figure was retrieved this session. `uptake` left empty.

### CA-ON|Ontario
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: Ontario's compulsory second language is French, an official language —
"French as a second language" (FSL) in three programs: Core French, Extended
French, French Immersion. Ontario's own genuinely-foreign-language stream is
called "classical and international languages" and is elective.

NEGATIVES / CORRECTION TO A COMMON CLAIM:
 - The Grade 4–8 French requirement and the 600-hour minimum are NOT in
   R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 298 (Operation of Schools — General), which is where they are
   often said to live. Term counts on the retrieved consolidated regulation text
   (on-reg298.txt, 62,843 chars): "core French" 0, "grade 4" 0, "grades 4" 0,
   "second language" 3 (all in teacher-qualification and definition clauses, e.g.
   s.19.1 and the s.1 definition). Sanity check on words that ARE present in the
   same file: "French" 19, "minutes" 9, "Sign Language" 6. The requirement is a
   Ministry curriculum-policy requirement stated in the 2013 FSL curriculum
   document, carried by the OS policy document — cite those, not Reg. 298.
 - Two OSSD tables appear in §6.1.1 (the pre-2024 requirements and those for
   students entering Grade 9 later, which add a technological education credit and
   a STEM group). Both list "1 credit in French as a second language"; the FSL
   requirement is unchanged between them.

# fl — Foreign languages in school | Canada: Prince Edward Island

### CA-PE|Prince Edward Island
STATUS: documented

FRAMING NOTE: PEI's own Program of Studies calls the subject "Core French" and, in
the Intermediate time-on-task table, "Other Official Language". It is never called a
foreign language. `Français langue première` under La Commission scolaire de langue
française is a separate minority-language system, not FSL. Mi'kmaq goes under
regionalMinorityLanguages.

 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - French is an official language of Canada, taught as a second language, not a foreign one
     - Second language courses in all schools, instruction beginning no later than grade four
     - Grades 4-6 time on task: Core French 10 per cent, a distinct special area
     - Grades 1-3 Core French exists only on a local pilot basis

 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Intermediate Grades 7-9 table names the subject Other Official Language, 11-13 per cent
     - Time allotment set by the Minister's Directive on Intermediate School Subject Time Allotments
     - Core French course codes run 7FREA, 8FREA, 9FREA across all three intermediate grades
     - Three immersion entry points: Kindergarten early, Grade 4 middle, Grade 7 late

 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - No second-language credit among the 11 compulsory credits for the 20-credit diploma
     - Compulsory list is 3 English, 2 math, 2 science, 2 social studies, 1 PE, 1 career education
     - Immersion students face the same graduation requirements as English-programme peers
     - French Immersion Certificate needs 6 French credits within the graduation parameters

 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - French is the only second language in the PEI Program of Studies at every key stage
     - Senior high Core French: FRE421A, FRE521A, FRE521G, FRE621A
     - Senior high immersion: FRE421F, FRE521F, FRE521G, FRE621F plus French-medium subjects
     - Core French pedagogy cites the Neurolinguistic Approach and the CEFR

 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Time allotments are based on a 300 minute instructional day
     - Elementary Core French 10 per cent, intermediate 11-13 per cent of the day
     - Late immersion: minimum 75 per cent of courses in French in Grades 7-8, 50 per cent in Grade 9
     - Early immersion: minimum 90 per cent French K-Gr 3, 65 per cent Grades 4-6 (2016 edition)

 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - DELF is optional and has no impact on the student's final mark in high school French
     - Offered at end of Grade 12 to students with at least one Grade 12 French course
     - Departmental initiative covers DELF for both French Immersion Grade 12 and Core French
     - No mandatory provincial oral proficiency exit assessment, unlike New Brunswick

 - field: teacherSupply

     - Education Act lets certification be refused for insufficient English or French proficiency

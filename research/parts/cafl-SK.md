### CA-SK|Saskatchewan

STATUS: documented

FRAMING NOTE: Saskatchewan's own categories are "French Immersion (Bilingual)",
"Fransaskois" (minority-language education under the Conseil scolaire
fransaskois) and "Core French" — which the ministry defines as "A second language
program in which French is taught as a subject or course". French is an official
language of Canada, not a foreign language, and the ministry's umbrella term is
"French as a Second Language (FSL) Programs". Indigenous language curricula are
recorded under regionalMinorityLanguages.

HEADLINE RESULT: There is NO compulsory second language anywhere in Saskatchewan
Kindergarten to Grade 12, and no language credit is required for a Saskatchewan
secondary diploma. This is sourced, term-counted and quoted below.

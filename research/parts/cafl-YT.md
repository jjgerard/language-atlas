### CA-YT|Yukon
STATUS: partial

FRAMING: Yukon has no curriculum of its own. It uses **British Columbia's**
curriculum, adapted, and follows BC's graduation requirements and assessment
schedule — which means BC's second-language rule (compulsory in Grades 5–8 under
BC Ministerial Order M295/95, no second language required for graduation) is the
operative rule in Yukon too. That inheritance is VERIFIED below from two
Government of Yukon news releases and must be cited, not assumed. French in
Yukon is an official language matter, not a foreign language. Yukon First
Nations languages are handled through the First Nation School Board's Land and
Language programming.

NEGATIVES:
 - Whole-page term counts on the Bahamas PEER profile body: speech=1, language=0, communicat=4,
   disabilit=31. "Language" appears ZERO times in the entire inclusion profile. The single
   "speech" is the Special Services Unit's screening remit.
 - The statutory test is "any disability of mind or body" — no communication or language category.
 - The named multidisciplinary team is special education teachers, psychologists, counsellors and
   school social workers. There is no speech-language therapist in it.
 - Art. 20.2 permits REJECTING a pupil with a disability from a regular school where facilities
   are unavailable and inclusion "can be seen as adversely affecting the learning of their peers".
   That is an unusually explicit exclusion clause and should be recorded.
 - No prevalence figure of any kind for language or communication need.

NEGATIVES:
 - Term counts on the Spanish profile body: logoped=2 (both in staffing lists), and there is NO
   separate definitional category for a language or speech disorder. *Logopeda* appears as a JOB,
   never as a diagnostic category with criteria.
 - No specific law regulates access of persons with disabilities to regular education
   ("no existe una ley específica"). A Ley de Educación was still forthcoming at the time of the
   profile ("prevista para el 2023") — its enactment status is NOT established here.
 - No language-specific prevalence figure exists.
 - The 2019 CRPD Committee criticised "la prevalencia de escuelas especiales" — i.e. the system's
   reliance on segregated settings — which should be recorded against serviceModel.

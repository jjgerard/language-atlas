NEGATIVES:
 - Dominica is the ONLY one of the six OECS units where PEER names "speech and language
   therapists" as support personnel in the education system in the generic present tense. No
   number, no employer, no register is given, and the Ministry's own two named teacher types do
   not include them.
 - The Act creates no speech/language category; the only hook is "communicative ...
   exceptionalities".
 - No monitoring or reporting mechanism, therefore no prevalence or outcome data.
 - Note the Education Regulations of 2011 put the initiating duty on the PARENT ("the parent
   shall in writing inform the Chief Education Officer"). That is a distinctive and citable
   identification rule.

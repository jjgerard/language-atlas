NEGATIVES:
 - Term counts on the DR PEER profile body: speech=0, languag=1, communicat=3, disabilit=45.
   The single "language" hit is "appropriate language" in the flexible-assessment clause. There is
   NO speech, language or communication disorder category in the Dominican framework as reported.
 - No provision was located on Haitian Creole-speaking children in identification or assessment,
   despite substantial Haitian migration. PEER's only cross-border item is a 2004 UNESCO-financed
   "Inclusive Schools Development Project" focused on schools in the border area.
 - The country's own ministry states its three disability-prevalence sources are not comparable.

DRAFT BULLETS:

   bullets:
     - Umbrella term is necesidades educativas especiales, also called "specific needs"
     - Nearest category is "developmental disorders" in Ordenanza 04-2018 Art. 1
     - That category is defined by severity of curricular adjustment, not by communication profile
     - "Speech" appears zero times in the whole UNESCO PEER inclusion profile
 - field: identificationCriteria
   bullets:

     - Default is mainstream enrolment from early childhood regardless of disability
     - Only severe and multiple disability routes a child to a special education centre

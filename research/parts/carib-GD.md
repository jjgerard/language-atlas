NEGATIVES:
 - No law explicitly prohibits disability discrimination in Grenada as at UNICEF's account
   reported by PEER, despite CRPD ratification in 2014.
 - No statutory speech/language category is attested; PEER does not quote the special education
   wording of either the 1976 or 2002 Act.
 - "Speech and language therapy" is named only as a service the Special Education Department
   offers, reported by UNICEF, with no staffing number.

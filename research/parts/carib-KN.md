NEGATIVES:
 - The Education Act 2005 contains "speech" zero times (sanity word school=554), even though the
   Ministry's Office for Special Education in practice offers speech and language therapy. The
   service exists without a statutory category behind it. This is the sharpest contrast in the set.
 - No prevalence figure for speech/language needs; PEER reports there is no data even on unmet
   special education need generally.

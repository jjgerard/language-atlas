NEGATIVES:
 - The Education Act Chap. 39:01 contains "speech" zero times (sanity word school=433). Its
   statutory category words are the 1966 vocabulary "deaf, mute, blind, retarded or otherwise
   handicapped". "Mute" is the nearest thing to a speech category in the statute.
 - All four of the Act's "communicat" hits concern communicable disease, records and
   communications, and communicating a Board decision — none is about communication disorder.
 - "Speech and language impairments" appears as a category of what special schools cater for,
   but only in the Ministry's own service description as reported by PEER, NOT in the statute.
 - There is no monitoring or reporting mechanism for inclusive education, so no outcome or
   prevalence series is available.

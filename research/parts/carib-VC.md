NEGATIVES:
 - "Speech impairment" appears once in the whole profile, and only inside a finding about
   SHORTAGE: there is "an acute shortage of trained staff, including therapists, for speech
   impairment". So the category is acknowledged in a workforce complaint, not in provision.
 - No record-keeping system for special needs identification (Ministry's own 2007 statement),
   therefore no prevalence data of any kind.
 - PEER reports the country "signed the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with
   Disabilities and its Optional Protocol in 2010" — signature, not ratification, as at the
   profile's 2021 revision. NOT re-verified against treaties.un.org in this session.

# Language Atlas — `eal` (Majority language acquisition) — Minnesota

TERMINOLOGY NOTE: the US term is **English learner (EL)**, not EAL. Minnesota's statute
defines "English learner" at Minn. Stat. 124D.59 subd. 2 and adds a distinct statutory
category, "English learner with limited or interrupted formal education" (SLIFE), at
subd. 2a. Use Minnesota's words.

ASSESSMENT NOTE: WIDA / ACCESS for ELLs named and linked only. Minnesota's statute refers
to "the state cutoff score on a commissioner-provided assessment" without printing a
number, so no cut-off is reproduced here either.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - US term is English learner, not EAL; Minn. Stat. 124D.59 subd. 2 defines it
     - Parent-declared home or first language other than English is the first limb
     - A valid English proficiency assessment plus developmentally appropriate measures
     - Districts set and document their own entrance criteria, applied uniformly
     - Separate statutory SLIFE category: two or more years behind peers on entry
 - field: removalCriteria
   bullets:
     - No single statewide exit cut: 124D.61 requires district criteria, documented
     - EL aid stops the year after the pupil attains the state cutoff score
     - Hard funding cap: seven years of average daily membership ends EL aid
     - Parents may withdraw a child from the EL programme at any semester's close
 - field: newcomerProportion
   bullets:
     - NCES Digest 2023 tbl 204.20, fall 2011: 70,225 ELs, 8.4% of enrolment
     - Fall 2016: 72,061 ELs, 8.2% of enrolment
     - Fall 2021: 76,664 ELs, 8.8% of enrolment
     - Statute requires reporting split by currently and previously counted ELs

     - Grade 4 mathematics gap is about 39 points

     - Districts must avoid isolating ELs for a substantial part of the day

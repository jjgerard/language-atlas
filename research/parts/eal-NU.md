# Language Atlas — `eal` (Majority language acquisition) — Nunavut

FRAMING NOTE — READ FIRST. Nunavut does NOT operate an EAL/ESL newcomer regime. It has no
newcomer designation at all. Instead the *Education Act* makes **bilingual education
compulsory for every student**, and fixes the languages of instruction by statute as the
Inuit Language plus either English or French. The question "what happens to a child who
arrives at school without the language that school teaches in?" therefore resolves into a
different question in Nunavut: a child may arrive without Inuktut, and the statutory
category that catches them is **"Inuit Language Arts — Inuktitut as a second language"**,
a phase-in track written into the Schedule to the Act. Do not write "no EAL designation";
write that there is no newcomer designation and name Nunavut's own categories.

TERMINOLOGY: the *Inuit Language Protection Act* was **renamed the *Inuktut Protection
Act*** and now says "Inuktut instruction" where it said "Inuit Language instruction".
Both wordings are documented below with their consolidation dates, because the map may
need either.

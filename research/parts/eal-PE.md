# Language Atlas — `eal` (Majority language acquisition) — Prince Edward Island

TERMINOLOGY NOTE: **EAL is PEI's own word**, and the province pairs it with **FAL, French
as an Additional Language**, because PEI runs an anglophone board (Public Schools Branch,
PSB) and a francophone board (La Commission scolaire de langue française, CSLF). The
department's own unit is called the "EAL/FAL section". The francophone side also uses
**francisation**. All three words are the province's, and all three should be recorded.

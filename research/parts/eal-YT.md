# Language Atlas — `eal` (Majority language acquisition) — Yukon

HEADLINE FINDING: **Yukon has no English-additional-language designation or policy of its
own.** This is a PROVEN absence, not a retrieval failure — the department's complete
published policy index and the CMEC guide covering the whole Yukon secondary system were
both retrieved and both term-counted to zero, against working sanity checks. What Yukon
does have is a **French First Language admission policy** and a heavy Yukon First Nations
language and culture apparatus. The "Yukon follows British Columbia" claim is VERIFIED for
curriculum, course registry, graduation and transcripts — but the BC ELL policy itself was
NOT retrieved and no Yukon document was found adopting it, so that specific link is
recorded as untested rather than confirmed.

THE "YUKON FOLLOWS BC" CLAIM — TESTED, PARTLY CONFIRMED:
 CONFIRMED for curriculum and credentials, from the CMEC guide (quotes below): Yukon uses
 BC's Course Registry, the B.C. Graduation Program Handbook of Procedures, the BC Ministry
 transcript and the Yukon/BC Dogwood Diploma.
 NOT CONFIRMED for language policy: no Yukon instrument adopting BC's English Language
 Learning policy was found, and BC's ELL policy guidelines were not retrieved this session.
 Do not assert that Yukon applies BC's ELL policy. Per brief rule 7, one jurisdiction's
 rule may not be inferred from a neighbour's.

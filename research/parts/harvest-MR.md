CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 Arabic is the official language and a medium of instruction in Mauritania, not a foreign language.
 PEER puts French, Fula, Soninke and Wolof under "Ethnicity and languages" too, and reserves the
 phrase "foreign languages" for a separate higher-education clause. Bullets below keep that.

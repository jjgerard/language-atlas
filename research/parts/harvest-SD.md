CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 Arabic is the medium of instruction in Sudan, not a foreign language, and PEER says so directly:
 "The official education language is Arabic from basic education upwards." English is described by
 PEER's own sources as a second language and a medium at higher levels, not as a taught foreign
 language. Bullets below keep those categories.

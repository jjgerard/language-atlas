WEAKEST UNIT IN THIS SET — READ BEFORE USING:
 The chapter is SHARED between Andhra Pradesh and Telangana and predates a clean
 separation of the two. Every recommendation is addressed to "The State
 Governments" in the plural and none is specific to Andhra Pradesh. Both
 governments filed no reply, so the chapter only carries forward the 51st Report's
 recommendations. Do not let Telangana's situation stand in for Andhra Pradesh's.

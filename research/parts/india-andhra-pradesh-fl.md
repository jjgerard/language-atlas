WEAKEST UNIT IN THIS SET — READ BEFORE USING:
 This chapter PREDATES A CLEAN SEPARATION of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. The
 Commissioner names both governments but still treats the two as one unit for
 every substantive purpose: the census table is headed "Andhra Pradesh/Telangana"
 and reports a single Census-2001 population of 7,62,10,007 for the undivided
 state, and the recommendations are addressed to "The State Governments" in the
 plural. NOTHING here describes Andhra Pradesh separately from Telangana, and
 Telangana's situation must NOT be allowed to stand in for Andhra Pradesh's.
 On top of that, both governments filed no reply, so the chapter only carries
 forward the previous (51st) Report's recommendations. Census figures are
 Census-2001; status is as at 2014-15.

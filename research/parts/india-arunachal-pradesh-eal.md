### IN-AR|Arunachal Pradesh — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented (a documented absence of provision, in a state with no majority language)
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind, and
Arunachal Pradesh uses none. The frame is the constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty
(Art. 350A). Arunachal is unusual in that the school and official language is ENGLISH while
no Census-2001 language reaches 20 per cent of the population — so almost every child arrives
at school without the language the school teaches in.

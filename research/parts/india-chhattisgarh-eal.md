### IN-CT|Chhattisgarh — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind. The
provision for a child who arrives without the language the school teaches in is the
constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty (Art. 350A) and the minority-language
medium-of-instruction facility the state reports against it. In Chhattisgarh the school
language is Hindi. English appears inside the Three Language Formula as a Special/General
option, not as a foreign language.

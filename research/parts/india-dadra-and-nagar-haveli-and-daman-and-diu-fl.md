CORRECTION TO THE TASKING NOTE:
 The task table recorded these two chapters as "RESPONDED". The chapter bodies say
 the opposite for the 52nd Report: BOTH UT Administrations filed NO REPLY to the
 52nd Report questionnaire. Daman and Diu's chapter cites a reply to the
 questionnaire for the 51st (previous) Report, which is probably the source of the
 confusion. Both non-reply sentences are quoted verbatim below.

### IN-DL|Delhi (NCT) — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind. The
provision for a child who arrives without the language the school teaches in is the
constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty (Art. 350A) and the minority-language
medium-of-instruction facility the state reports against it. In Delhi both Hindi and English
are official languages of the NCT and both are media of instruction; English is not a
foreign language here.

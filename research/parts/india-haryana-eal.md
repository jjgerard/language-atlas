### IN-HR|Haryana — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: partial (an explicit, documented absence — the state supplied no medium-of-instruction data)
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind. The
provision for a child who arrives without the language the school teaches in is the
constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty (Art. 350A) and the minority-language
medium-of-instruction facility the state reports against it. In Haryana the school language
is Hindi; English is the additional official language of the state, not a foreign language.

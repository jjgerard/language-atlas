### IN-KL|Kerala (India) — map `eal` (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented

TERMINOLOGY NOTE (per the brief): the school language in Kerala is **Malayalam**, and English is
stated by the state to be the medium of instruction at higher secondary level. "EAL" is not the
term in play and is not used below. Chapter 33 names no newcomer, induction or additional-language
designation; the provision for a child who arrives without Malayalam is Tamil-medium or
Kannada-medium schooling from class I to class X.

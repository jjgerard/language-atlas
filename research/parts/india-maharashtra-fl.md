DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - As at 2014-15 the state filed no reply to the Linguistic Minorities Commissioner
     - Official language of the state is Marathi (CLM 52nd Report, para 30.2)
     - Census-2001: Marathi 68.79%, Hindi 11.03%, Urdu 7.12%, Gujarati 2.39%
     - Hindi, Gujarati and Urdu academies existed; the Sindhi Academy was defunct
     - CLM asked the state to provide facilities for learning tribal languages
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Marathi compulsory as a subject, Standards I to X, phased from 2020-21
     - Standard I in 2020-21, then one standard added each year to V by 2024-25
     - Applies to CBSE, CISCE, IB, IGCSE, Cambridge, NIOS and MIEB schools alike
     - Applies to minority schools under Art. 30(1), aided or unaided
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Standard VI in 2020-21, then one standard added each year to X by 2024-25
     - Teaching and assessing Marathi is a condition of recognition or NOC
     - Existing schools not teaching Marathi have recognition cancelled, s.4(2)
     - Penalty up to one lakh rupees on the person managing a defaulting school
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Every school must follow the curriculum the Government specifies, s.5
     - Assessment of pupils in Marathi must be conducted in all schools, s.3(3)
 - field: policyHistory
   bullets:
     - 2015 CLM 52nd Report records no Maharashtra reply to its questionnaire
     - 2020 Maharashtra Act III makes Marathi compulsory in all schools, Std I-X

### IN-MN|Manipur — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind, and
Manipur uses none. The frame is the constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty (Art. 350A).
Manipur teaches through BOTH English and Manipuri from Class I to X, so a child from one of
the hill-district tribal languages arrives without either school language; the state's answer
is the tribal language as a SUBJECT, not as a medium.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - No newcomer designation; the frame is mother-tongue instruction under Art. 350A
     - English and Manipuri are both media of instruction and examination, Class I to X
     - Thirteen recognised tribal languages are taught as subjects, never as media
     - Poumai, Liangmei and Gangte stop at Class VIII; eight others run to Class XII
     - Language Preference Registers not yet working; effort focused on Hill Districts

     - No sanctioned posts exist for minority-language teachers anywhere in the state
     - Teachers whose own mother tongue matches are assigned to teach the language
     - Textbooks free under Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, with language Literature Societies

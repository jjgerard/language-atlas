DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - As reported by the state to the Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities, 2014-15
     - Formula runs from Class III to Class X
     - First: Manipuri, a major Indian language, or one of 13 recognised tribal languages
     - Second Language: English
     - Third: Manipuri or Hindi, whichever the pupil did not take as first language
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - As reported to the CLM, 2014-15
     - Manipuri is the official language; English is the only other official language
     - English and Manipuri are both media of instruction and examination, I to X
     - Thirteen recognised tribal languages are taught as subjects from Class I
     - Poumai, Liangmei and Gangte run only to Class VIII
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - As reported to the CLM, 2014-15
     - English and Manipuri remain the media of instruction to Class X
     - Mao and Rongmei run as subjects to Class X; eight tribal languages to XII
     - National rule, NCF 2023: three languages, at least two native to India
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - As reported to the CLM, 2014-15
     - English alone continues as the medium of instruction beyond Class X
     - The Three Language Formula itself is stated to stop at Class X
     - Kom, Vaiphei, Mizo, Zou, Tangkhul, Hamar, Thadou-Kuki and Paite run to XII
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - Census-2001 shares, excluding three Senapati sub-divisions not enumerated

     - 36 Scheduled Tribes, mostly in the five Hill Districts
     - Textbooks by the Board of Secondary Education with language Literature Societies
     - No language academy has yet been set up in the state

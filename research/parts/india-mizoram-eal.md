### IN-MZ|Mizoram (India) — map `eal` (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: partial — the provision that exists is documented, but Mizoram furnished no information
on several heads and the CLM says so on the page

TERMINOLOGY NOTE (per the brief): the school language in Mizoram is **Mizo**, with English and
Hindi also official. "EAL" is not the term in play and is not used below. Neither the chapter nor
any instrument it names creates a newcomer, induction or additional-language designation. The
provision for a child who arrives without Mizo is structural: Bengali-medium and Nepali-medium
schools, concentrated at the primary stage.

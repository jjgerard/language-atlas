### IN-TR|Tripura (India) — map `eal` (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented — and the headline finding is a NEGATIVE that the state itself stated four
times over

TERMINOLOGY NOTE (per the brief): the school language in Tripura is **Bengali**, with English and
Kokborok also official. "EAL" is not the term in play and is not used below. The chapter names no
newcomer, induction or additional-language designation.

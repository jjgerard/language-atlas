### IN-UP|Uttar Pradesh — map: eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: documented (a documented restriction rather than a documented provision)
NOTE ON TERMS: India has no newcomer or additional-language designation of the EAL kind. The
provision for a child who arrives without the language the school teaches in is the
constitutional mother-tongue-instruction duty (Art. 350A) and the minority-language
medium-of-instruction facility the state reports against it. In Uttar Pradesh the school
language is Hindi; Urdu is the additional official language of the state, and English sits in
the second slot of the state's Three Language Formula.

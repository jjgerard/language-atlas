 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Uttar Pradesh furnished NO stage-by-stage data to the CLM for 2014-15
     - Hindi is the official language; Urdu is the additional official language
     - Even the count of recognised minority institutions was not supplied
     - National rule, NCF 2023: R1 is the language most familiar to the student
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Board rules constrain Urdu, as reported to the CLM, 2014-15
     - Urdu may be taken as a subject at Secondary and Intermediate
     - Board syllabus rules bar pairing Urdu with Science, Maths, Commerce or Sanskrit
     - 1952 Board notification: recognition only for Hindi-medium institutions
     - CLM calls that notification contrary to Articles 29 and 30
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Not found: the state furnished no higher secondary information to the CLM
     - The Urdu-pairing bar applies to Intermediate as well as High School
     - National rule, NCF 2023: two languages at XI-XII, one native to India
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:

     - Urdu Academy budget dwarfs the others at Rs 6.20 crore in 2014-15

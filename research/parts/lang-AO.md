LANGUAGE NOTE: Chokwe (ISO cjk), Kimbundu (kmb), Nyaneka (nyk) and Kwanyama (kua) have no WALS record on name or ISO code. The source's 'Olunyaneka' and 'Oshikwanyama' are given here without their noun-class prefixes.
LANGUAGE NOTE: Kikongo resolves as WALS 'Kongo' [kon, iso kng]. NOTE THE TRAP: WALS code 'kng' is Kaingang, a Je language of Brazil.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - Two terms coexist: 'Angolan languages' and 'languages of African origin'
     - The curriculum slot itself is called simply 'a national language'
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Law 13/01 admitted them as a medium; Portuguese remains it in practice
     - Textbooks in seven Angolan languages were trialled from 2008
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - The primary curriculum carries Portuguese plus one national language
     - Which one depends on the dominant language of that part of the country
     - Reported as still at the experimental stage as of late 2014

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - "Lenguas Originarias" is MINEDUC's own heading for them
     - Speakers are "pueblos originarios" whose "lengua vernácula" is still current
     - The ministry's word for the Mapuche language is "mapuzugun"
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Not a medium: the four are taught as a subject, not used to teach other subjects
     - Delivered by a "dupla pedagógica" of classroom teacher and educador/a tradicional
     - Ley 20.370 arts. 28-30 reach parvularia, básica and media
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Asignatura Lengua Indígena, four hours a week
     - Compulsory where indigenous enrolment exceeds 20%, optional elsewhere
     - Two axes only: oralidad, and written code using Spanish graphemes
     - Schools may add their own, e.g. lengua chedungun, mapuche-huilliche

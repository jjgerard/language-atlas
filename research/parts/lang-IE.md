DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - Irish is a state language, not a regional or minority one, Eurydice Fig. A1
     - Ireland recognises no regional or minority language in the Charter sense
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Irish-medium primary schools exist; the exemption rules do not reach them
     - Circular 0052/2019 applies to English-medium primary schools only
     - Eurydice files English-plus-Irish CLIL as two state languages, ISCED 1-3
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Compulsory: the primary language curriculum is for all pupils, Irish and English
     - Exemption possible in exceptional circumstances, Circular 0052/2019
     - Exemptions granted under the old Circular 12/96 run to end of post-primary

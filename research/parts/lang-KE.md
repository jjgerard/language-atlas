DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - 'the language of the catchment area' - a territorial rule, not a named list
     - Swahili is a national and official language, not a minority one
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - The catchment-area language is the medium in Grades 1 to 3
     - In practice English is used as the medium even in Grade 1
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - English and Swahili are both taught as subjects alongside it
NOTE: The policy is deliberately open-ended: it names no language, only the rule that the school uses the language of its catchment area. Only Swahili is named, and as a subject.

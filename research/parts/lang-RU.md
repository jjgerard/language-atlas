DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - Two categories: 'rodnoy yazyk' and 'gosudarstvennyy yazyk respubliki'
     - A rodnoy yazyk must be one of the languages of the peoples of the Federation
     - Russian itself may be chosen as a rodnoy yazyk under the 2018 amendment
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Right to preschool, primary and basic general education in the rodnoy yazyk
     - Exercised by creating the necessary schools, classes and groups
     - Bounded by 'the possibilities provided by the education system'
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - A republic may introduce teaching of its own state language
     - Such teaching may not be to the detriment of teaching Russian

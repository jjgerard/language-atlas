DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: localTerm
   bullets:
     - Two distinct categories: 'mova korinnoho narodu' and 'mova natsional'noi menshyny'
     - Indigenous-people status carries the wider right of the two
     - The 2023 amendment narrowed the classroom right to EU official languages
 - field: mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Indigenous-people languages reach preschool and all general secondary
     - National-minority languages reach preschool and primary only
     - Delivered through separate classes or groups, always alongside the state language
     - The right does not extend into Ukrainian-medium classes
 - field: taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Right to study the language in municipal general secondary schools
     - Or through national cultural societies, as an alternative route
NOTE: NO LANGUAGE ROWS WRITTEN. Article 7 names no specific language: it works entirely through the two categories. Ukraine's indigenous peoples are designated by a separate 2021 law which this entry does not cite and which was not read here.

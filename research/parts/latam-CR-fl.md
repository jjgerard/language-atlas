### CR|Costa Rica — fl (Foreign languages in school)
STATUS: documented

HEADLINE: Costa Rica has the longest and densest foreign-language policy chain in the batch —
1994 primary programmes, 1995 experimental bilingual liceos, a 2008 Plan Nacional de Inglés and
Costa Rica Multilingüe, the 2018 Alianza para el Bilingüismo (ABi), a 2021 Política Educativa de
Promoción de Idiomas with a 2021-2040 action plan, plus a compulsory school-leaving language test.
It ALSO has the batch's most candid published evidence that the chain has underdelivered.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Foreign-language programmes in primary date from 1994
     - A 2019 Directriz de Bilinguismo targeted improving English at preschool level
     - 2022 Orientaciones set out an immersion model for foreign language in preschool
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Experimental bilingual liceos were created in 1995
     - Bilingual schools deliver half the provision in Spanish and half in English or another language
     - Bilingual sections were expanded to "democratise" bilingual education
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Target is hedged in the source itself as holding "en un contexto ideal"
     - Plan de Accion 2021-2040 aims at B1 or B2 for secondary leavers by 2040
     - Most secondary students currently graduate below the MEP's B1 minimum
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Six languages are offered: English, French, Mandarin, Portuguese, German and Italian
     - Indigenous languages sit in the separate indigenous subsystem, not this menu
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Applies to public bilingual liceos only, not to all schools
     - Those institutions teach 25 to 60 per cent of lessons in the foreign language
     - Minimum rises from 14 lessons a week to at least 32 by the highest level
     - The total is reached by teaching maths or science through the foreign language
 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - The national English exam in force from 1985 tested reading comprehension only
     - Decreto Ejecutivo 41686 of 28 February 2019 replaced it
     - Prueba de Dominio Linguistico ran in 2019, 2021 and 2022 and was required for Bachillerato
     - TEYL, first applied in 2021, bands II Ciclo pupils at pre-A1, A1 or A2
 - field: teacherSupply
   bullets:
     - Teacher supply is the binding constraint on expanding bilingual provision
     - Some schools have English teachers teach maths or science instead
     - Resolucion DG072-2018 rewrote the English specialty in the civil service manual
     - Bilingual secondary teachers must reach at least CEFR B2 in their subject
 - field: higherEducation
   bullets:
     - UCR's PELEX delivers the national school English test under a MEP agreement
     - UCR's Escuela de Lenguas Modernas administers the TEYL
     - Dedicated bilingual teaching degrees were still only a proposal in 2024
 - field: uptake
   bullets:
     - Figures attributed to a 2019 government communique, not retrieved directly
     - 2019: 92,000 pupils taking English, against 1,650 French and 1,200 Italian
     - Mandarin 350, Portuguese 325 and German 40 pupils in the same year
     - 2017 household survey: only 10 per cent of Costa Ricans master a second language
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - Indigenous schools run Programas de Lenguas y Culturas Indigenas in cycles one and two
     - Decreto 37801-MEP recognises Cabecar, Bribri, Ngobe, Bugle and Maleku as current
     - Teribe and Boruca are classed as undergoing revitalisation

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - Umbrella term is "necesidades educativas especiales" (SEN), no separate language category
     - Ministry SEN definition names "cultural or linguistic" cause, meaning disadvantage not disorder
     - PEER profile has zero hits for speech, language disorder or language therapy
     - Specialized education is a distinct legal category under 2018 regulations
 - field: identificationCriteria
   bullets:
     - Ley Organica de Discapacidades 2012 requires a comprehensive assessment before special placement
     - Assessment must be requested or approved by the parent or legal representative
     - It is carried out by a multidisciplinary team specialising in disability
 - field: referralPathway
   bullets:
     - 140 District Inclusion Support Units (UDAI) assess and route pupils
     - UDAI decide between specialised education and ordinary inclusive education
     - No language-specific referral route was found
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Under-Secretariat of Specialized and Inclusive Education runs the national system
     - Art. 28 Ley Organica de Discapacidades allows specialised temporary or permanent staff
     - It also allows communication and learning-space accessibility adaptations
     - National Centre for Educational Resources for Diversity supplies materials
 - field: legalEntitlement
   bullets:
     - LOEI 2011 and Ley Organica de Discapacidades 2012 are the two entitling statutes
     - Special school placement is permitted only where mainstream cannot accommodate the pupil
 - field: identifiedPrevalence

     - Ministry of Education: 80,368 school-age people with disability-related SEN, 78 per cent in school

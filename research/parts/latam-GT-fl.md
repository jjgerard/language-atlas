### GT|Guatemala — fl (Foreign languages in school)
STATUS: documented

HEADLINE: Guatemala does not have a "foreign language subject" bolted on to a monolingual
curriculum. It has a THREE-SLOT LANGUAGE ARCHITECTURE — L1 / L2 / L3 — written into the
*áreas fundamentales* of the Currículo Nacional Base from grade 1 of primary, in which the
FOREIGN language is structurally the THIRD language, after the mother tongue and a second national
language. Record the categories exactly as the CNB assigns them.

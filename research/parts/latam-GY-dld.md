DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - Anglophone-Caribbean vocabulary applies: the term in use is "special education needs"
     - The 2014 Education Bill definition names persons "mute, deaf, blind or otherwise... challenged"
     - No developmental language disorder category exists in Guyanese education law
     - Entitlement hangs on the word "communicative" in the chief education officer's duty
 - field: legalEntitlement
   bullets:
     - Caveat: PEER calls the 2014 instrument a Bill, and enactment was not verified
     - Chief education officer must provide a special education programme for eligible pupils
     - Eligibility covers intellectual, communicative, behavioural, physical or multiple attributes
     - Delivery must be in the least restrictive environment that resources permit
 - field: identificationCriteria
   bullets:
     - The school principal decides, with professional staff and the parent
     - Multidisciplinary assessment is required only "if possible"
     - Written parental consent is required before any assessment
 - field: referralPathway
   bullets:
     - School Health Unit screening is the main identification route
     - Special Education Unit sits within NCERD and sets policy
     - A national special education needs coordinator sits in the central ministry
     - Parents have a right of appeal to the minister
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Support may take the form of an individual education plan
     - Placement follows a least-restrictive-environment test qualified by available resources
     - Special education classes are established in schools under the 2010 Persons with Disabilities Act
 - field: funding
   bullets:
     - IEP cost is apportioned between the parents and the ministry, not fully state-funded
 - field: workforce
   bullets:

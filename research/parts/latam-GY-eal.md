### GY|Guyana — eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: partial

TERMINOLOGY NOTE: Guyana IS English-medium and IS Anglophone Caribbean, so "EAL" is a legitimate
vocabulary here per the shared brief. But the retrieved Guyanese sources do not actually use it —
the Ministry of Education frames the issue as MOTHER-TONGUE support for indigenous/hinterland
children, and PEER calls it "the language issue". Prefer the ministry's own framing.

### HN|Honduras — fl (Foreign languages in school)
STATUS: partial

HEADLINE NEGATIVE, AND IT IS THE INTERESTING ONE: Honduras's national curriculum makes a foreign
language compulsory but NEVER NAMES ENGLISH. Term count on the retrieved Currículo Nacional Básico
(2003): **"inglés" = 0**, against "lengua extranjera" = 9 and "idioma extranjero" = 4.
SANITY CHECK on the same file: "Comunicación" = 38, "lengua materna" = 13. The CNB is
language-neutral by design; English enters Honduran schooling through a SEPARATE and largely
PRIVATE bilingual-schools sector and through bilingual teacher-training colleges, not through the
core curriculum instrument.

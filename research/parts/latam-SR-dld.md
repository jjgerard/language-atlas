DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - Suriname has no inclusive education law and no official definition
     - The 2008 special-education report lists handicaps and behavioural problems, not language
     - Dutch "logopedie" is the term that would apply; it is absent from education policy
 - field: legalEntitlement
   bullets:
     - No statutory entitlement: PEER finds no law or policy framework on inclusive education
     - The 1987 Constitution Art. 39 guarantees free primary education and equal opportunity only
     - Suriname ratified the CRPD on 29 March 2017, after National Assembly approval in 2015
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - The Pedologisch Instituut implements special education programmes nationally
     - A Health Care Network for Special Education supports pupils needing special education
     - Special schools are concentrated in Paramaribo (18); five districts have none
     - No school-based speech and language service was found

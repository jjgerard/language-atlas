### SR|Suriname — eal (Majority language acquisition)
STATUS: partial

TERMINOLOGY NOTE (this unit is the unusual one in the batch): Suriname is DUTCH-MEDIUM. The question
"what happens to a child who arrives at school without the school language" is here about DUTCH, not
English and not Spanish. Do NOT write "no EAL designation". The relevant Dutch terms are
*instructietaal* (language of instruction), *Nederlands als tweede taal* and *hulptaal* (auxiliary
language). The languages around the school language are Sranan Tongo (the national lingua franca),
the Maroon languages (Saramaccan, Aukan/Ndyuka) and the Indigenous languages, plus Sarnami
Hindustani, Surinamese Javanese and Chinese.

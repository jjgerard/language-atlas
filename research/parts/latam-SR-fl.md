### SR|Suriname — fl (Foreign languages in school)
STATUS: partial

TERMINOLOGY NOTE: Suriname is DUTCH-MEDIUM. Dutch is the school language, not a foreign language.
The foreign languages in the Surinamese examination system are ENGLISH (compulsory) and SPANISH
(in named subject packages). French is NOT an examination subject at HAVO or VWO. Do not import an
English-as-the-foreign-language framing from Central America without noting that Dutch sits above it.

     stripping the zero-width characters that pervade the file:
       Engels = 0, Spaans = 0, Frans = 0, "vreemde taal" = 0, Nederlands = 0, Sranan = 0,
       instructietaal = 0, moedertaal = 0, taalbeleid = 0.
     SANITY CHECK on the same cleaned text: onderwijs = 451, leerlingen = 71, taal = 56 (all inside
     compounds such as meertaligheid), meertalig = 6.
     CONCLUSION: Suriname's current national education policy plan contains NO foreign-language
     policy at all, and does not name a single language — not even Dutch. What it does carry is
     MULTILINGUALISM as a cross-cutting principle. The plan records that "Meertaligheid" was one of
     eight position-paper themes prepared for the October 2023 education congress, and commits to
     "Curriculumvernieuwing in het basisonderwijs met nadruk op leerjaar 9 en 10, integratie van
     Basic Life Skills, meertaligheid, speciaal onderwijs en maatwerk voor rurale context"; teacher
     professionalisation programmes are likewise to address "actuele onderwijstrends, meertaligheid,
     digitale [vaardigheden]". Anyone reporting a Surinamese foreign-language strategy from this
     plan would be reporting something that is not in it.
   source: https://gov.sr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Nationaal-Onderwijsbeleidsplan-Suriname-2024-2031-DEF-1-Correct.docx.pdf
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages

     Suriname" — i.e. languages as CULTURAL HERITAGE CONTENT, not as taught languages.

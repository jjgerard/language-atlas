### SV|El Salvador — fl (Foreign languages in school)
STATUS: documented

HEADLINE: El Salvador is the CEFR-anchored case in this batch. Its English syllabi are built
explicitly on the Common European Framework, they carry an official hour allocation (96 hours), and
they set a national exit target (A2+ at the end of Educación Media). Crucially, the rollout is
STAGED AND PARTIAL: the first-cycle materials went to a NAMED SUBSET of 1,143 schools in 2023, and
the third-cycle/media materials were, at time of publication, still under validation.

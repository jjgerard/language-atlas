DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - "Trastornos de lenguaje" is one of nine areas de atencion set by the 1976 policy document
     - Specialist training pairs it with hearing as "Deficiencias Auditivas y Problemas de Lenguaje"
     - The 1976 policy expressly rejects aetiological categories for a psychopedagogical profile
     - Later instruments use "necesidades educativas especiales" without a language sub-category
 - field: identificationCriteria
   bullets:
     - Assessment is educational and psychological, producing a functional profile
     - The stated aim is to avoid nosological labels prejudicing educational intervention
     - Placement decisions require a multidisciplinary team
 - field: referralPathway
   bullets:
     - Centres for Diagnosis, Guidance, Training and Monitoring of Disability created in 2011
     - An interdisciplinary group evaluates the pupil at these centres
     - 153 centres nationwide by 2015 against a stated target of 335
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Special education runs as a "modalidad" parallel to the basic education subsystem
     - 2011 reform converted special schools into Bolivarian Schools and technical schools
     - The 2013 school inventory lists no school type for language
 - field: legalEntitlement
   bullets:
     - Resolucion 2005 of 1996 obliges all schools to admit and retain SEN pupils
     - Ley para Personas con Discapacidad 2007 Art. 16 gives a right to attend an institution
     - Art. 20 guarantees deaf pupils bilingual education in Venezuelan Sign Language and Spanish
 - field: workforce
   bullets:
     - Specialist teachers, not therapists, are the trained workforce named in policy
     - Training began September 1976 at the Instituto Pedagogico de Caracas
 - field: identifiedPrevalence
   bullets:
     - Figure is all-impairment special-education enrolment, not language-specific
     - 40,296 pupils enrolled across special education settings in 2013

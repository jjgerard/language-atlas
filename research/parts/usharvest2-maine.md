### US|Maine
STATUS: documented
TERMINOLOGY: Maine's guidance uses "multilingual learner (ML)" and "English learner"; the
programme is ESOL. Not "EAL". Maine's home language survey is called the Language Use Survey.

 - Maine's guidance is 38,477 characters as extracted. Occurrences: "bilingual" 0, "two-way" 0,
   "transitional" 0. "dual language" occurs once and only as the title of a WIDA PreK
   professional-learning resource ("Resources and professional learning specific to PreK Dual

   programme. Present in the same file: "English learner" 14, "translat" 5, "interpret" 4,
   "home language" 3, "newcomer" 1. Maine therefore has substantial home-language ACCESS

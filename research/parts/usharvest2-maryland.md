ABSENCE PROVED - this is the finding for l2Support:
 - Maryland's guidance is 13,422 characters as extracted and, unlike Alabama, Kentucky, Maine,
   Nevada, Rhode Island, South Carolina and Utah in this same batch, it contains NO
   "EL Services or Program Requirements" section at all. The document runs from process
   overview and home language survey through assessments, exit criteria, test administrator
   training and website permissions, and stops. Occurrences: "bilingual" 0, "dual language" 0,
   "translat" 0, "interpret" 0, "preferred language" 0, "pull-out" 0, "push-in" 0. Present in
   the same file: "English learner" 6, "home language" 4, "English language development" 2.
   Maryland's teaching-of-English arrangements are therefore not documented by the source cited
   on this entry; what IS documented is identification, assessment and exit.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Districts must provide bilingual education or English as a second language, Educ Law 3204
     - Bilingual programmes give content instruction in the child's native language and English
     - Transitional Bilingual Education keeps home-language instruction throughout, not only early
     - Dual Language runs one-way or two-way, commonly 50/50, sometimes 90/10 narrowing over time
     - Stand-alone ENL serves pupils with no shared home language group for a bilingual class

 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Placement figures are IDEA Part B child-count data, not a therapy-delivery audit
     - Speech-language pathology is a related service, 8 NYCRR 200.1(qq)
     - Resource room gives supplementary instruction, individual or small group, part of the day
     - Consultant teacher service is delivered inside the pupil's regular education classes
     - 100,250 of 147,526 school-age speech or language pupils in regular class 80%+ of day

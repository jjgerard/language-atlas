MOST IMPORTANT FINDING - the cited federal guidance is rescinded:
 The ED-hosted PDF of the 2015 Dear Colleague Letter now opens with a stamp reading, verbatim:
 "RESCINDED: THIS DOCUMENT HAS BEEN FORMALLY RESCINDED BY THE DEPARTMENT AND REMAINS AVAILABLE
 ON THE WEB FOR HISTORICAL PURPOSES ONLY." The document does not state the date of rescission,
 so no rescission year is asserted here. This matters far beyond the US entry: the ECS 50-State
 Comparison's entire "Federal Law" row is sourced to this letter, and several state rows say
 the state is "Governed by ... federal law rather than state policy".

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer or additional-language designation exists in Ley 26.206
     - Art. 143 guarantees migrants without a DNI access, permanence and completion
     - Foreign documents from the country of origin must be accepted for enrolment
     - The only bilingual modality is Educacion Intercultural Bilingue, for indigenous
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Educacion Especial is a MODALIDAD across every level, governed by inclusion
     - Art. 43 requires early identification of disability or developmental disorders
     - Attention is interdisciplinary; specialised staff team with mainstream teachers
     - Provinces set the procedures and resources; the national law fixes no caseload
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - At least one foreign language is COMPULSORY in all primary and secondary schools
     - Art. 87 leaves strategy and timetable to Consejo Federal de Educacion resolutions
     - It names no particular language, so the choice is not fixed nationally
     - A secondary-level aim is to understand and express oneself in a foreign language

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Speech therapists sit on the school-entry team with pedagogues and psychologists
     - First professional observation is usually before primary enrolment, by the social work centre
     - That centre performs the categorization and assessment of abilities
     - Preschool inclusion covers children with mild speech-voice communication disorders
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Neither Eurypedia page describes any home-language provision at all
     - 'Children with special needs' is the working category and it is not legally defined
     - National minorities, returnees and refugees fall under it only rarely
     - So the country's support architecture is a disability one, not a language one
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Neither cited page names a language-support measure for new arrivals
     - Individual adapted teaching programmes follow an expert opinion, on disability grounds
     - The procedure is not defined for preschool institutions at all
     - Enrolment of students in secondary school is not precisely defined either
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer criterion is stated in any of the three sources
     - Eurydice records under 5 per cent of 15-year-olds in language-heterogeneous schools
     - PISA samples for at least one immigrant-background category were under 30 students
     - Bosnia and Herzegovina is outside several Eurydice figures entirely

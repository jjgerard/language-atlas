DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - No bilingual programme: a bridge from the native language to Standard English
     - The native language is the starting point but is not a medium of instruction
     - Work concentrates on oral language in the early years, before literacy
     - PEER's Barbados profile names no ethnic or linguistic minority provision
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Regional exam syllabus, NOT proof of a national requirement in one territory
     - CSEC Modern Languages offers exactly two languages: French and Spanish
     - Recommended load: five 40-minute periods a week over the last two years
     - All candidates sit a 10-15 minute oral in the target language, Paper 03

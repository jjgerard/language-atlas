DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Alberta uses EAL, not ESL, and writes home language INTO the proficiency benchmarks
     - At the earliest levels descriptors expect reliance on home language and translation
     - Parents are encouraged to keep developing the home language, as skills transfer
     - Home-language proficiency is named as a factor in the rate of English development
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Benchmarks 2.0 is a proficiency scale, not a programme model, so it authorises no model
     - It permits home-language translation as scaffolding within an English-medium classroom
     - Pre-K to K benchmark assessment covers the listening and speaking strands only
     - Alberta's assessment-supports page now redirects to the LearnAlberta curriculum home

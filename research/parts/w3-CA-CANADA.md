NOTE: This is a federal-level entry. The pattern to record is that Canada's federal language-in-education instruments are about the two OFFICIAL languages, not about the majority-language acquisition question the eal map asks. Reading the Protocol as an EAL instrument would be a category error.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Careful: in Canadian federal usage 'second language' means the other OFFICIAL language
     - The CMEC Protocol defines minority language and second language as English and French only
     - Federal funding addresses official-language education, not newcomer language support
     - Provinces hold exclusive jurisdiction over education, so EAL provision is provincial
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - The federal first-language right is Charter s.23 official-language minority schooling
     - It applies at primary and secondary level where numbers of students warrant
     - It covers English or French minorities, not immigrant or Indigenous home languages
     - Quebec does not subscribe to the CMEC Protocol and negotiates bilaterally instead
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - OLA s.43(1)(e): Canadian Heritage assists provinces on learning both English and French
     - This is second-OFFICIAL-language teaching, distinct from EAL for arriving students
     - The CMEC Protocol channels the money through bilateral federal-provincial agreements
     - Section 43 was last amended by 2023, c. 15, s. 22 (Bill C-13)
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - No pan-Canadian model exists; British Columbia is given here as the documented example
     - BC funds speech-language pathology out of the Basic Allocation, not a supplemental category
     - BC's eight supplemental Inclusive Education categories have no speech or language one
     - Supplemental funding requires assessment, identification and a current IEP
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - No national upper-secondary language requirement: education is exclusively provincial
     - The federal instrument recognises the importance of learning English or French as L2
     - It funds provincial second-language instruction programmes rather than setting them
     - Current instrument is the 2024-2025 to 2027-2028 CMEC Protocol

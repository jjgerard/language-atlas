DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer or additional-language designation exists in Ley 115 de 1994
     - TERM COUNT in Ley 115: migrante 0 and migratorio 0, against educacion 546
     - Its bilingual provision, art. 57, is for ethnic groups with a linguistic tradition
     - In those territories the mother tongue is the school foundation, by statute
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - The delivery instrument is the PIAR, a Plan Individual de Ajustes Razonables
     - It rests on a pedagogical and social assessment and feeds classroom planning
     - Funding: an extra 20 per cent per disabled student reported in SIMAT
     - Territories may create temporary docente de apoyo pedagogico posts
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - In educacion media all areas are obligatory; foreign languages can be intensified
     - Humanidades, lengua castellana e idiomas extranjeros is an obligatory area
     - Ley 1651 de 2013 amended the objectives on foreign-language expression
     - The Act names no particular foreign language

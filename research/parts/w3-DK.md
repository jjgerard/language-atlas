METHOD NOTE:
 This is a research instrument, not a national nomenclature. What it evidences is the wording the
 country's own COST Action representatives chose when they rendered the Action's agreed definition
 of "language impairment" into Danish. It is quoted here as that and nothing more. The English
 master version calls the same construct "Language Impairment (LI)" (p. 210).

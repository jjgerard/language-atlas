DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Dominica's primary curriculum is the OECS harmonised Language Arts, not its own
     - It offers second language or second dialect approaches for Creole speakers
     - Teachers of French Creole speakers read a Kweyol story daily, then retell it
     - The stated goal is fully functioning bilingual and bi-literate students
 - field: fl.primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Kweyol in primary is a pilot, not a requirement: 19 schools from 2019
     - The OECS harmonised primary Language Arts curriculum names no foreign language
     - A Ministry draft Creole curriculum was reported in October 2025, still draft
     - Reported by news outlets, not by a ministry circular retrieved here
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Kweyol is framed as heritage and identity, not as a foreign language
     - KEK's stated long-term goal is Creole in all primary and secondary schools
     - Materials exist: ANNOU APWANN KWEYOL plus three Creole dictionaries
     - Dominica State College and UWI Open Campus already run Creole courses
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Regional exam syllabus, NOT proof of a national requirement in one territory
     - CSEC Modern Languages offers exactly two languages: French and Spanish
     - Recommended load: five 40-minute periods a week over the last two years
     - All candidates sit a 10-15 minute oral in the target language, Paper 03

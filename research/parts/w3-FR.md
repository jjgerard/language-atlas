METHOD NOTE:
 This is a research instrument, not a national nomenclature. What it evidences is the wording the
 country's own COST Action representatives chose when they rendered the Action's agreed definition
 of "language impairment" into French. It is quoted here as that and nothing more. The English
 master version calls the same construct "Language Impairment (LI)" (p. 210).

     - Term below is the French COST IS1406 survey translation, not a statutory label
     - French version uses “trouble du langage (TL)” throughout
     - Primary form is “primaire”, used when the origin of the disorder is unexplained
     - Secondary form is “secondaire”, tied to a wider condition such as cerebral palsy

WHAT THE SOURCE DOES NOT SAY:
 The technical note is a funding document. It defines who attracts EAL money and how much; it says nothing about what schools must do with it, and nothing about first-language maintenance or bilingual instruction. The bullets are written so that a reader cannot mistake a funding factor for a programme.

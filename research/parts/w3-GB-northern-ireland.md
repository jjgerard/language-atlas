DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - The 2007 Order fixes minimum content, not a list of named languages
     - At Key Stage 3 there are two language areas: Language and Literacy, and Modern Languages
     - Language and Literacy is specified as 'English with Media Education'
     - Modern Languages carries its own minimum content and names no particular language
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - The local word is 'newcomer'; the service is the Intercultural Education Service
     - Support is organised centrally by the Education Authority rather than school by school
     - The retrieved page is a service landing page and describes no home-language teaching
     - 'Newcomer' here is the pupil category; EAL is not the term this system uses

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - The OECS curriculum tells teachers to ACCEPT contributions in the Creole
     - The child's language is a stepping-stone to literacy, not an error to remove
     - Guidance covers both English-lexifier and French Creole speaking children
     - Regional harmonised curriculum; Grenada is named on its cover as a member
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - The curriculum leaves any Creole-medium policy to each member state
     - Grenada is not documented as having taken that option in sources read here
     - The stated aim is bilingual competence built from the child's own language
     - Second language or second dialect approaches use the same tailored activities
 - field: fl.primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - The OECS harmonised primary Language Arts curriculum names no foreign language
     - TERM COUNT: Spanish 0, French 7 (all French Creole), against language 202
     - Grenada is a named member state of that harmonised curriculum
     - No Grenada-specific primary foreign-language requirement in the cited sources
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - At primary the languages in play are English and Creole/CIV, not foreign ones
     - The upper-secondary offer is CSEC Modern Languages: French and Spanish
     - No Grenada national language-offer list was retrievable from cited sources
     - The SUMMA Grenada country review adds no foreign-language subject data
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Regional exam syllabus, NOT proof of a national requirement in one territory
     - CSEC Modern Languages offers exactly two languages: French and Spanish
     - Recommended load: five 40-minute periods a week over the last two years
     - All candidates sit a 10-15 minute oral in the target language, Paper 03

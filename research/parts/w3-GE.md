DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Right runs to citizens whose native language is not Georgian, not to arrivals
     - They may acquire a complete general education in their native language
     - Provision is by non-Georgian-speaking institution or sector, not by pupil
     - Multilingual primary teachers teach subjects in different languages in those sectors
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Non-Georgian-speaking sectors run inside general education institutions
     - Multilingual teachers of basic and secondary school teach subjects in various languages
     - Bilingual teaching principles are mandatory where only deaf pupils are educated
     - Georgian sign language is a language of instruction in those settings
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Teaching the official language is mandatory in every native-language school
     - In the Autonomous Republic of Abkhazia both official languages are mandatory
     - Certain subject groups must be taught in Georgian even in those schools
     - No separate second-language programme or timetable is set by the Law
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer or second-language pupil category exists in the Law
     - 'newcomer' and 'second language' each return 0 hits in the English text
     - The default medium is Georgian, or Georgian or Abkhazian in Abkhazia
     - The trigger is citizenship plus a native language other than Georgian
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - The Law names no specific foreign language anywhere
     - A foreign language may be the medium under an international agreement
     - Otherwise foreign-medium teaching needs the Ministry's agreement
     - Cited matsne links are document records, not the curriculum annex text
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - No speech or language domain appears in Georgia's assessment instruments
     - Medical assessment domains are vision, hearing, motor, mental and intellectual
     - Child assessment uses a 57-question Child Functioning Assessment Tool
     - A Functional Assessment Specialist administers it, answered by the parent

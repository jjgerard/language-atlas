DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Bahasa Indonesia is the medium of instruction in national education
     - A local language may be the medium only in the early stage of schooling
     - The Elucidation fixes that early stage as grades 1 and 2 of primary school
     - No Bahasa Indonesia as a second language route is set out in the Law
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - The criterion is regional, not individual: how often the language is used there
     - 'newcomer', 'mother tongue' and 'second language' each return 0 hits in the Law
     - No pupil is designated as a second-language learner anywhere in it
     - Local-language teaching at basic level follows regional frequency of use
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - The Law names no specific language beyond Bahasa Indonesia
     - A foreign language may be a medium to support learners' competence in it
     - 'Language' is one of ten compulsory curricular areas at basic and secondary level
     - Content detail is delegated to Government Regulation under art. 37(3)
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - The Law sets no upper-secondary language rule and names no language
     - 'Language' is a compulsory curricular area for secondary education as a whole
     - It is also compulsory in higher education under art. 37(2)
     - The entry needs a curriculum regulation before this field can be filled
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Terapi Wicara is regulated as a health-care profession, not an education service
     - Its scope is language, speech, voice, rhythm/fluency and swallowing
     - It is delivered to individuals, families or groups
     - The SIKTW work permit authorises practice in health-care facilities

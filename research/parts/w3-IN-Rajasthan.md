 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - State furnished no reply to the Commissioner for 2014-15, so provision is unverified
     - Regional/State language introduced not earlier than class III if different from mother tongue
     - Regional language a compulsory subject throughout the Secondary Stage (1949 Resolution)
     - Pupils may answer in mother tongue for the first two years after the Junior Basic Stage
     - No second-language pupil category: the safeguard secures mother-tongue medium instead
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - Category is 'linguistic minority': mother tongue differs from the State's principal language
     - Mother tongue is the language declared by the parent or guardian
     - Threshold: 40 pupils of that tongue in a school, or 10 in a class, gets one teacher
     - At secondary, one-third of a school's pupils may request mother-tongue instruction
 - field: fl.primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - 1961 formula was adopted for the Secondary stage, not as a primary-stage rule
     - Regional/State language enters not earlier than class III where it differs from mother tongue
     - It must enter no later than the end of the Junior Basic Stage
     - CLM reports a trend of States making regional languages compulsory at primary stage
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - State furnished no language list to the Commissioner for 2014-15
     - Formula limb 1: the regional language, and the mother tongue where it differs
     - Formula limb 2: Hindi, or in Hindi-speaking areas another Indian language
     - Formula limb 3: English or any other modern European language
     - Mother tongue may be studied as one of the three languages at secondary stage
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Regional language compulsory as a subject throughout the Secondary Stage
     - SRC 1956 declined constitutional recognition of mother-tongue medium at Secondary
     - Mother-tongue-medium secondary school allowed where pupil numbers justify one
     - CLM collects Three Language Formula enrolment for Class XII as well as VIII and X

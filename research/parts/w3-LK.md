DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - The other national language is taught, not the medium: this is second-national-language policy
     - Oral Sinhala or Tamil from Grade 1, then as a subject from Grade 3
     - English enters orally from Grade 1 and as a subject from Grade 3
     - Cited outcome data: about 90% of Sinhala speakers cannot communicate in Tamil
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer category: entitlement is to be educated in either national language
     - Constitution art. 21(1) gives that entitlement to every person
     - Pupils enter a Sinhala-medium or a Tamil-medium school accordingly
     - 71.5% of government schools are Sinhala medium and 27.8% Tamil medium
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Sinhala and Tamil are the national languages; English is the link language
     - Every pupil takes the other national language as a subject from Grade 3
     - English is the third language for all
     - Bilingual streams teach a few subjects in English and the rest in Sinhala or Tamil
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Neither cited source states a school-level rule for upper secondary
     - 'upper secondary' returns 0 hits in Jhingran 2019 while 'primary' returns 181
     - Constitution art. 21(2) reaches state-financed universities, not schools
     - There it requires the other national language be made a medium too
 - field: dld.terminology
   bullets:
     - The Ministry's data-collection category is 'speech disorder', with no language category
     - It is one of 17 disability categories on the National Student Information System form
     - The class teacher identifies the 'special need' or 'difficulty' against that list
     - 'language disorder' returns 0 hits in the cited UNICEF profile
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - One training route only: BSc Speech and Hearing Sciences, University of Kelaniya
     - It specialises in either Speech & Language Therapy or Audiology
     - Placements run across health, education and rehabilitation settings
     - Practice requires Sri Lanka Medical Council registration

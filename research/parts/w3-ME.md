TERMINOLOGY:
 The law's own phrase is 'crnogorski jezik kao nematernji jezik', Montenegrin as a non-mother-tongue, and 'maternji jezik' for the pupil's own language. Those are the words used, not EAL.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Montenegrin is the school language; the statutory category is minority language, not EAL
     - Schools teaching Montenegrin as a non-mother-tongue get two extra lessons a week
     - End-of-cycle testing is in Montenegrin or in the mother tongue, whichever applies
     - Roma and Egyptian pupils have literacy guidelines and a Roma-Montenegrin picture dictionary

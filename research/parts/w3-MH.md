DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Two languages only: Marshallese (Kajin Aelon Kein) and English
     - Both are taught as subjects at every grade level, K to 12
     - Marshallese is medium for all subjects except English Language Arts in K-6
     - No third or foreign language appears in the policy
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Grades 7-12 run a 50/50 bilingual arrangement of the two languages
     - Social science, health, PE and art are taught and assessed in Marshallese
     - Mathematics and science are taught and assessed in English
     - Each language gets 60 minutes a day as a subject in grades 9-12

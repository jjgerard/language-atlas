CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 North Macedonia teaches through Macedonian and through other languages of instruction, so the question here is not EAL. The law's own terms are 'мајчин јазик' (mother tongue) and 'јазикот на кој ја следи наставата' (the language in which the pupil follows instruction).

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - The statutory word is 'мајчин јазик', mother tongue, and instruction runs in several languages
     - Returning pupils must be helped with mother tongue and language of instruction
     - Schools organise individual and group supplementary teaching for exactly those two
     - Foreign or stateless children get remedial Macedonian and subject support for at least a year
 - field: dld.terminology
   bullets:
     - Term below is the Macedonian COST IS1406 survey translation, not a statutory label
     - Macedonian version uses “нарушување на говорот (НГ)”, disturbance of speech
     - One of the few translations that says speech where the English said language
     - The primary and secondary split is kept: примарно or секундарно

CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 Chichewa and English are both official languages of Malawi and both are media of instruction at different stages; neither is recorded here as a foreign language.

CONTRADICTION TO FLAG:
 The SASLHA institution list names no Malawian university offering speech therapy or audiology training, which agrees with the ENT survey's blank speech-training column for Malawi. Where a map entry says otherwise, both sources should be shown.

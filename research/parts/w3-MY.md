DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No pupil-level category: the trigger is a parental request, not a child's language
     - Malay is the main medium in all institutions bar national-type schools
     - Where the medium is another language, Malay is a compulsory subject
     - Chinese or Tamil must be taught if the parents of fifteen pupils request it
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - The Act defines the stage but sets no upper-secondary language rule
     - Upper secondary is education for a pupil who has completed lower secondary
     - s. 17(2) makes Malay compulsory at every stage where the medium is another language
     - English is a compulsory subject of instruction in national schools by definition

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Ley 29735 makes learning castellano a RIGHT, as the common-use language
     - It sits beside the right to education in one's mother tongue and own culture
     - The law is about lenguas originarias, not the vocabulary of newcomers
     - It also gives a right to be served in one's mother tongue by state bodies
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer or additional-language designation exists in Ley 29735
     - TERM COUNT in the law: migrante 0 and extranjer 0, against lengua 79
     - Originary languages are co-official with castellano where they predominate
     - The question this law answers is indigenous-language, not immigration

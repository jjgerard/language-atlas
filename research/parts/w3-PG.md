DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Four official languages: English, Tok Pisin, Hiri Motu and sign language
     - Elementary vernacular can be any of about 850 tokples, Tok Pisin or Hiri Motu included
     - Oral English enters in the second half of Elementary 2
     - Since 2015 schools are to teach both a vernacular and an English elementary syllabus
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Grades 11 and 12 were added to selected high schools after the Matane Report 1989
     - The 1-6-6 structure makes Grade 12 the single exit point of school education
     - Neither cited source names a language subject requirement for Grades 11-12
     - English is the medium by upper secondary, having been bridged to from lower primary
 - field: policyHistory
   rows:
     - {year: 1989, description: Matane Report restructuring introduces elementary schooling in the community language and adds Grades 11 and 12 to selected high schools}
     - {year: 2003, description: PNG national curriculum implements the vernacular language policy; elementary Language syllabus issued}
     - {year: 2015, description: Elementary standards-based curriculum introduces a separate English syllabus teaching English as a second language alongside the vernacular Language syllabus}
     - {year: 2016, description: NEC Decision No. 315/2016 adopts the 1-6-6 school structure}
     - {year: 2020, description: NEC Decision No. 347/2020 approves the National Education Plan 2020-2029}

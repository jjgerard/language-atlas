DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer category: learners are classified by mother tongue via language mapping
     - DepEd must implement a language mapping framework with the KWF
     - A monolingual class is a group sharing a mother tongue in the same graded level
     - Mother language is defined by identification and by which language the child knows best
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Since October 2024 the medium reverts to Filipino and English
     - Regional languages serve as auxiliary media of instruction only
     - Mother-tongue medium survives as an option in monolingual classes
     - That option requires a KWF orthography, documented vocabulary and a grammar book
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Secondary education is four years of junior and two of senior high school
     - The 2013 Act aimed at Filipino and English as primary media at secondary level
     - Its Grade 4-6 transition programme is the route to that point
     - Neither cited Act sets a language subject requirement for senior high school
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Statutory definition covers hearing, language and speech processes together
     - Speech-language pathology is a Related Service under the 2022 inclusion Act
     - Inclusive Learning Resource Centers deliver it through a multidisciplinary team
     - The 2019 Act made speech language pathology a licensed profession

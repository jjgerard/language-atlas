DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Instruction must START in the pupil's official mother tongue, from first grade
     - The right extends to an indigenous language as well as guarani or castellano
     - Ley 4251 states it as an individual right, not a discretionary provision
     - The pupil's language, not the school's, decides which language is L1
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - A guarani-castellano bilingual plan is required across the WHOLE system
     - It runs from educacion inicial through to educacion superior
     - Separate differentiated plans are required for indigenous peoples
     - This is a two-official-language model, not a transitional one
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - The OTHER official language is taught from the start, alongside the first
     - Art. 31 requires second-language didactics for it, not L1 methods
     - Which language is L1 and which L2 depends on the pupil, not the school
     - Paraguay's L2 question is guarani-castellano, not English
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer or additional-language designation in Paraguayan education law
     - TERM COUNT: migrante 0 and inmigrante 0 in both Ley 1264 and Ley 4251
     - The frame is pluriculturalidad: two official languages plus indigenous ones
     - Ley 1264 art. 75 only recognises foreign-language institutions officially
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Ley 5031/2013 puts English in the public curriculum to Tercero de la Media
     - That is the final upper-secondary year, so the mandate spans all of schooling
     - It also starts at Preescolar, below primary
     - The law took effect three years after promulgation; rollout is gradual

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: dld.terminology
   bullets:

     - Words for the field: taxatub (communication), nutq wa lugha (speech and language), kalaam
     - Arabic is the medium of instruction in Sudan, so no additional-language framing applies
     - No Sudanese statutory or ministry term was reachable from this entry's one cited link
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - This is an absence stated by the editorial's authors, not a Sudanese government statement
     - They are unaware of any university speech-language therapy programme in Sudan
     - The 2015 ENT survey records no audiology and no speech training programme in Sudan
     - Sudan reported 2 speech therapists in 2015 and had no 2009 figure

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - The 'second language' in Singapore's own usage is the Mother Tongue, not English
     - MTL is a compulsory subject; the three official MTLs are Chinese, Malay and Tamil
     - Non-Tamil Indian Languages are examinable but not taught by MOE
     - Those classes run outside school premises and hours
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - Assignment is by ethnicity, not by a language test or a newcomer status
     - Double-barrelled race defaults to the first component, with a right to apply otherwise
     - Eurasian and other-race pupils may request any official MTL, subject to approval
     - Long residence overseas is one ground for taking a Foreign Language instead
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Approved Foreign Languages: French, German, Japanese
     - Approved Asian Languages: Arabic, Burmese, Thai
     - These must be prepared privately for the GCE O-Level at the end of secondary
     - MTL requirements attach to progression to the next level of study

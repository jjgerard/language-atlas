DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - No Thai-as-a-second-language provision appears in the National Education Act
     - 'mother tongue', 'second language' and 'bilingual' each return 0 hits in it
     - The Act requires emphasis on proper use of the Thai language for all
     - Its only relevant category is s. 10 'communication deficiencies'
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - No bilingual programme is named in the Act: 'bilingual' returns 0 hits
     - The nearest provision is the s. 27 power over local curricular substance
     - That power is framed around local wisdom, not around language of instruction
     - Institutions prescribe it to meet the needs of the community and society
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - The Act creates no newcomer or additional-language pupil category
     - 'newcomer', 'migrant' and 'mother tongue' each return 0 hits in it
     - The nearest category is the s. 10 list, which includes communication deficiencies
     - Those pupils have a right to basic education specially provided
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - The field is named ความผิดปกติของการสื่อความหมาย, communication disorders
     - The practitioner title is นักแก้ไขการพูด, speech-language pathologist
     - The profession began in university hospitals and remains hospital-based
     - Since B.E. 2545 practitioners must be licensed as practitioners of the healing arts

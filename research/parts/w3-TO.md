DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Tongan and English for all, with optional French, Japanese and Mandarin
     - Tongan is the main medium to end of Year 3; bilingual in Years 4-6
     - English becomes the main medium from Year 7 (Form 1) onwards
     - Education Act 2013 s. 48(f) makes promoting both languages a curriculum principle
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Tongan Language and culture is taught in all schools from Years 1 to 13
     - English-medium schools must still teach Tongan Language in every year
     - Competence in oral and written English is the target by end of secondary
     - That is Class 1 to Form 7, so both languages run to the end of school
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Framework treats language learning difficulties as a policy issue, not a service
     - It promises diagnosis and support for language learning difficulties
     - Diagnostic instruments were to be used early in primary to find literacy problems
     - Pacific review lists speech pathologists as a needed specialist, not a present one

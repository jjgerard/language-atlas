DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: fl.primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - The SIS page documents a national SAFFL policy but names NO primary rule
     - Listed services: ministry courses, teacher workshops, translation, Spanish Day
     - SAFFL was created by Cabinet Directive of 9 September 2004
     - The SIS target date of 2020 passed with no primary mandate in evidence
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - The cited guide covers Forms 1-3, where Spanish gets 3 periods a week
     - English Language Arts and Mathematics get 7 periods each in the same table
     - Above Form 3 the documented route is CSEC Modern Languages, French and Spanish
     - No Forms 4-6 timetable allocation was retrievable from cited sources

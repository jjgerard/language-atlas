CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 kiSwahili and English are both official languages of Tanzania. kiSwahili is the primary medium and English the secondary medium; the 2014 policy is the one document that puts English among 'other foreign languages', and that wording is reported as the source's, not adopted here.

   bullets:
     - 1995 Education and Training Policy: English a compulsory subject in all seven primary years
     - Kiswahili is the medium in pre-primary and primary, so it is not a foreign language
     - Policy expected pupils to reach the English proficiency needed for post-primary by Standard 7
     - 2014 policy, launched February 2015, restates capacity building in several languages
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - 2014 policy names Kiswahili, English and 'other foreign languages' without listing them
     - English is the only additional language the sources name concretely at school level
     - The 1997 Cultural Policy promised Kiswahili as medium at all levels, never implemented
     - Tibategeza and du Plessis call English 'the second or foreign language in Tanzania'
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - 1995 policy: English is the medium for secondary education, Kiswahili a compulsory subject
     - The 2015 announcement extends Swahili-medium basic education into four secondary years
     - Both sources agree English has remained the post-primary medium in practice
     - 1982 Makweta commission had proposed Kiswahili medium from Form One by January 1985
 - field: dld.serviceModel

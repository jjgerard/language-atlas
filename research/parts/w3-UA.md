DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Ukrainian is the state language of the educational process, so this is not an EAL system
     - Indigenous peoples and national minorities have a right to study their language at school
     - That right may also be met through national cultural societies rather than the school
     - Deaf pupils have a right to be taught in sign language and to study Ukrainian Sign Language
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Minorities: separate classes in the minority language alongside the state language
     - That runs to preschool and primary only, and not in Ukrainian-medium classes
     - Indigenous peoples: the same arrangement, but through general secondary education
     - Subjects may be taught in two or more languages including English and EU official languages
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Institutions must ensure compulsory study of the state language
     - Proper conditions for learning it are guaranteed to minorities, foreigners and stateless persons
     - Vocational and higher institutions must reach a level usable in professional work
     - The law sets no separate newcomer programme, only this general guarantee
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer designation exists: the criterion is minority or indigenous status, not arrival
     - Since the 2023 amendment the guarantee attaches to EU official minority languages
     - Article 7 is the whole of the law's language architecture, with details left to special laws
     - Sign language is treated separately, as a right of pupils with hearing impairment
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Since the June 2024 amendment the state must ensure the study of English
     - It also promotes study of other languages of international communication
     - That duty applies to institutions of every form of ownership
     - Higher and vocational students may request a minority-language subject of their own

CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 English and kiSwahili are Uganda's official languages; English is the medium from Grade 4. Neither is recorded here as a foreign language.

CONTRADICTION TO FLAG:
 The 2015 ENT survey records 0 speech therapists in Uganda, while Bowen's directory records a Makerere BSc in Speech and Language Therapy whose first graduates qualified in 2010 and a Speech and Language Therapy Department at Mulago Hospital, and the SASLHA institution list also names Makerere. The ENT survey figure is what one national ENT respondent reported; it is not a register, and its speech column should not be read as a count of the profession.

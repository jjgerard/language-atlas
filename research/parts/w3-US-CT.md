DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Bilingual education is capped at thirty months, extendable by thirty more on request
     - After that, boards must provide language transition and academic support services
     - Named options: ESL, sheltered English, English immersion, research-based development
     - Late secondary arrivals with under thirty months to graduation go straight to ESL
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Primary or sole speech-language disability makes the service SPECIAL EDUCATION
     - Where speech-language is secondary to another disability it is a related service instead
     - The SLP need not be the sole or even primary provider of direct services
     - SLP may use support staff or consultative service but keeps design and supervision

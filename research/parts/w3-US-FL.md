DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Fla. Stat. 1003.56 allows home language instruction in the basic subject areas
     - Basic subjects named: reading, mathematics, science, social studies, computer literacy
     - The alternative is ESOL instruction in those same subjects, at district choice
     - Home language is defined by the parents' normal language, not only the student's
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - SLP joins the IEP whether language service is special education or a related service
     - Providers: certified SLP, licensed SLP, or a speech-language associate under direction
     - The speech-language associate route runs three years and only in sparsity-supplement districts
     - Districts must file a service-model plan with the Department before using associates

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Hawaii treats speech-language pathology as special education when it is the disabling condition
     - Needing a related service alone does NOT make a student a student with a disability
     - But needing speech-language pathology services does trigger that determination
     - Effect: speech-language need alone is sufficient for an IEP in Hawaii

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Trigger is 20 English learners of one language classification in an attendance centre
     - A full TBE programme must teach maths, science and social studies in the home language
     - Under 20 speakers, districts may run TBE or a locally determined transitional programme (TPI)
     - A TPI must give native language support to the extent needed, plus ESL instruction
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Statutory caseload cap: a full-time SLP may not exceed 60 students at any time
     - Speech-language pathology assistants may not hold a caseload of their own
     - Hiring an assistant does not change the supervising SLP's caseload limit
     - Parents must be notified in writing when an assistant will serve their child

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Bulletin 741 lets LEAs staff EL programmes with bilingual personnel as well as EL teachers
     - Home-language support to families is required: translated materials or a trained interpreter
     - The rule does not require or fund first-language instruction for the student
     - Each LEA must adopt an EL policy whether or not it currently enrols any ELs
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Louisiana prescribes no bilingual model: the LEA designs a programme reflective of needs
     - ECS records Louisiana as governed by the EL guidebook or federal law, not state policy
     - That federal basis is weaker than it looks: the 2015 EL Dear Colleague Letter is rescinded
     - LEAs must still meet the OCR and DOJ standards for evaluating an EL programme
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Special education evaluation may NOT be delayed for language proficiency or EL programme
     - EL classification cannot itself be the basis for finding a disability
     - Speech or language impairment criteria sit in Bulletin 1508, the Pupil Appraisal Handbook
     - Bulletin 741 sets no speech-language caseload cap

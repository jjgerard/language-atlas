DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Plan triggered at 10 same-language learners in K-3, 20 in grades 4-8, 20 in grades 9-12
     - The threshold counts learners of the SAME language in one school, not the district
     - Meeting Wis. Stat. 115.95 makes the district eligible for partial state reimbursement
     - Districts may seek exemption to hire ESL-certified rather than bilingual teachers
 - field: dld.serviceModel
   bullets:
     - The IEP team must include an SLP licensed under ch. PI 34
     - For an English Learner it must ALSO include an educator versed in L2 acquisition
     - Impairment must be shown within the child's own home language, culture or dialect
     - PI 11.36(5) was repealed and recreated effective 1 August 2021

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Policy 2417 treats primary languages as 'funds of knowledge', a resource not a deficit
     - ELP standards expect learners to draw on knowledge of morphology in their native language
     - West Virginia's EL category expressly includes ELs with significant cognitive disabilities
     - The policy provides no first-language medium of instruction, only first-language leverage
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Policy requires a research-based ALTERNATIVE language programme, not bilingual education
     - ECS cites W. Va. Code R. 126-15-3 for that requirement
     - Aim of the programme is English proficiency plus the academic content standards
     - Policy 2417's curriculum applies regardless of the student's native language

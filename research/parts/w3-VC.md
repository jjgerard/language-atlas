   bullets:

     - No bilingual education provision is documented for SVG
     - The shared OECS primary curriculum leaves a Creole-medium policy to each state
     - SVG is not documented as having taken that option in sources read here
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - No national list was retrievable; the documented offer is the CSEC pair
     - CSEC Modern Languages offers French and Spanish, with a compulsory oral
     - The OECS harmonised primary curriculum names no foreign language subject
     - SVG is a named member state of that harmonised primary curriculum
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Regional exam syllabus, NOT proof of a national requirement in one territory
     - CSEC Modern Languages offers exactly two languages: French and Spanish
     - Recommended load: five 40-minute periods a week over the last two years
     - All candidates sit a 10-15 minute oral in the target language, Paper 03

CATEGORY DISCIPLINE:
 English, Shona and Ndebele are all officially recognized languages of Zimbabwe. Gudhlanga and Makaudze's phrase 'valued a foreign language' is their characterisation of colonial policy and is quoted as such, not adopted as this entry's category for English.

CONTRADICTION TO FLAG:
 The 2015 ENT survey records 1 speech therapist in Zimbabwe and no speech training programme, while the University of Zimbabwe's own page lists a BSc Honours Speech and Language Therapy degree and SASLHA lists the same department. The university page carries no start date, so the two are not necessarily inconsistent in time; both are given.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: eal.newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No pupil-level newcomer or second-language designation found in national law or curriculum
     - Policy targets areas, not pupils: 国家通用语言文字教育基础薄弱地区, 国发〔2015〕46号
     - Compulsory Education Law art 12 routes school access by hukou, not by language
     - 教语用〔2021〕4号 puts pupils' Putonghua level into national quality monitoring
 - field: eal.l2Support
   bullets:
     - Support is front-loaded into pre-school, not delivered as in-school withdrawal teaching
     - 国家通用语言文字法 art 11: schools teach in the national common language, in force 2026-01-01
     - Same article: learners must basically master it by the end of compulsory education
     - 童语同音 pre-school Putonghua plan targets ethnic areas, 教语用〔2021〕4号
     - 民族区域自治法 art 37: minority-medium schools open a 汉语文 course in primary school
 - field: eal.l1Support
   bullets:
     - Provision is territorial: it attaches to ethnic autonomous areas, not to individual pupils
     - 民族区域自治法 art 37: minority-majority schools may use minority textbooks and teach in them
     - Governments must fund compiling and publishing minority-script textbooks, art 37 para 4
     - 国家通用语言文字法 art 18 allows dialect use where genuinely needed in teaching
     - UN CERD 2018 reported restriction of Tibetan-medium and Mongolian-medium schooling
 - field: eal.bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - 双语教育 is the term of art, pairing the national language with a minority one
     - 教育法 art 12, 2021 amendment: minority-majority schools in autonomous areas do 双语教育
     - Model One (一类模式) taught all subjects but Chinese in the minority language
     - Unified state textbooks for 语文/道法/历史 phased in: 5 regions 2017, 6 more 2020
     - 国家通用语言文字法 art 11 now requires nationally unified textbooks in all schools
 - field: fl.primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - English is compulsory from grade 3, 义务教育课程方案 2022, 教材〔2022〕2号
     - Well-resourced areas and schools may start in grades 1-2, listening and speaking first
     - Foreign language is 6-8% of the nine-year compulsory total of 9522 taught hours
     - Grades 1-2 are shaded out of the 外语 row in 表2 of the 2022 curriculum plan
 - field: fl.upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Foreign language is compulsory in senior secondary: 6 of the 88 compulsory credits
     - Passing the 学业水平合格性考试 on compulsory courses is a key basis for graduating
     - Foreign language is one of the three gaokao subjects with Chinese and maths, 国发〔2014〕35号
     - The gaokao foreign-language paper offers two sittings, 国发〔2014〕35号
 - field: fl.languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Six languages on paper at senior secondary, only three in compulsory education
     - Compulsory education: English, Japanese, Russian only, 义务教育课程方案 2022
     - Senior secondary adds German, French and Spanish, 普通高中课程方案 2017/2020
     - Gaokao foreign-language paper: one of English, Russian, Japanese, French, German, Spanish
     - Schools pick their own first foreign language; a second is encouraged, not required

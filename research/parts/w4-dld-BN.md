DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - No local diagnostic category for language disorder found on any Brunei government page
     - Protected title is Speech and Language Therapist, Allied Health Professions Act Cap 221
     - Health service is named "Speech and Language Therapy" at the Child Development Centre
     - Schools work from "special educational needs"; nearest label is "communication" impairment
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - Health-sector model: the Child Development Centre at Kiarong is the paediatric hub
     - CDC pairs speech and language therapy with audiology, OT, physio and psychology
     - Therapists must hold AHPCBD registration; the Act took effect on 1 July 2017
     - Schools deploy SENA special-needs teachers; no school-based therapist post was found
POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 2017
   description: Allied Health Professions Act Cap 221 in force, making speech therapy a registered profession
 - year: 1997
   description: Special Education Policy introduces SENA teachers in mainstream schools (PEER, unvalidated)

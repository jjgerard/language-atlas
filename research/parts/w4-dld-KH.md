CAVEATS:
 - The IJSLP paper's full text is paywalled; every quote above is from the freely visible abstract.
 - The OIC figures are the organisation's own estimates. The workforce numbers (13 facilities, 27
   practitioners, 0.16:100 000) come from the peer-reviewed abstract and are dated to that study.
DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - No settled clinical term: the speech therapy profession is not yet established in Cambodia
     - Recognised disability types run physical, vision, hearing, intellectual, psychosocial, autism
     - No speech, language or communication category appears among those recognised types
     - Main provider's working English term is "language impairment", the pre-CATALISE label
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - NGO- and private-led: a study found 27 practitioners across 13 facilities
     - Workforce density reported at 0.16 per 100,000, urban services far ahead of provincial
     - There is no Cambodian university training programme in speech-language pathology
     - Services are limited in scope, accessibility and integration into government systems

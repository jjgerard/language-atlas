 - field: terminology
   PROSE (the atlas's third state):
     Not established from the sources consulted. No Lao term for a childhood language disorder was
     found, and no category that a child with DLD would fall into. The UNESCO PEER profile for Lao
     PDR reports that "A definition of special education needs has not been found", and the
     operational definition it does record — from the 2011-15 National Strategy and Plan of Action on
     Inclusive Education — is built from social groups (girls and women, ethnic children, learners
     with disabilities, remote populations), not from types of impairment. A peer-reviewed 2020
     account of cleft-palate speech services states there is no speech-language pathologist
     profession in the country, which is consistent with no clinical vocabulary having been settled.
     Searched: the UNESCO PEER Lao PDR inclusion profile, the 2020 IJPORL paper and its PubMed
     record, and general web search for speech and language therapy terminology and services in
     Lao PDR. No Lao Ministry of Education or Ministry of Health page naming such a category was
     located.

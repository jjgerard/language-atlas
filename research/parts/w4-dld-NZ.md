ABSENCE FINDING:
 - The ORS Guidelines (January 2025) contain ZERO occurrences of "developmental language disorder",
   "DLD" or "specific language impairment" (checked by case-insensitive grep of the extracted
   document text). Neither MoE page read in this session names a diagnostic category either. The
   CATALISE consensus is therefore NOT reflected in the Ministry documents read here. DLD is used in
   New Zealand professional and academic material, but that was not verified from an official
   Ministry or NZSTA policy source in this session and is not asserted below.
DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - Ministry of Education describes needs functionally and assigns no diagnostic label
     - Working term is "speech, language and communication needs", not DLD or SLI
     - Te reo on the MoE page: "hapainga korero, hapainga reo, hapainga whakawhiti korero"
     - ORS gate needs "a severe disorder of both language use and social communication"
     - ORS guidelines never use "developmental language disorder" or "language disorder"

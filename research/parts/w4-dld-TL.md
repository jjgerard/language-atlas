   PROSE (not bullets — the atlas's third state):
     Not established from the sources consulted. The Lei de Bases da Educação (Lei n.º 14/2008)
     entitles children with limitations of speech, language and communication to "respostas
     educativas adequadas" and says special education is delivered by teachers "e outros técnicos
     especializados", but names no profession and no setting. The published service list of the
     Hospital Nacional Guido Valadares, the country's tertiary referral hospital, contains no speech
     therapy line — its nearest entries are Medisina Rehabilitasaun and ENT. The only account found
     of a Timorese speech pathologist is a visiting Australian clinician's undated first-person piece
     for the International Communication Project, which describes a single practitioner based in Dili
     working alongside visiting cleft surgical missions. Searched: hngv.ms.gov.tl (Ministry of Health
     national hospital), the Jornal da República text of the education framework law, the UNESCO PEER
     Timor-Leste inclusion profile (itself marked "Validated by the country: No"), and general web
     search for speech and language therapy provision in Timor-Leste.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: serviceModel
   bullets:
     - School-based: district-employed SLP, amount of service set by the IEP committee
     - MDE caps a full-time school SLP's caseload at 48 students
     - Two MDE licences: 215 AA full scope, 216 A limited to articulation therapy only
     - Named delivery models are pull-out, push-in, consultation, team teaching and teletherapy

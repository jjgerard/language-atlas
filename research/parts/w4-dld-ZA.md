ABSENCE FINDING:
 - Neither "developmental language disorder", "DLD" nor "specific language impairment" appears as a
   category in the SIAS 2014 policy text read in this session; the nearest named domain is
   "Communication", defined as above. CATALISE terminology is used in South African journal
   literature (SAJCD 2022) but was not found in the national school policy documents read.
DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: terminology
   bullets:
     - Policy category is "learners who experience barriers to learning", a deliberately broad framing
     - White Paper 6 chose "barriers to learning and development" over "special education needs"
     - SIAS domain "Communication" is scoped to little or no functional speech, requiring AAC
     - No DLD or SLI category in SIAS; CATALISE terms appear in SA research, not in school policy

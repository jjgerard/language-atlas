DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - Domestic minority-medium schooling, not migration, is the question here
     - 2010 Constitution: Kyrgyz is the state language, Russian holds official language status
     - Art 10.3 recognises every ethnicity's right to preserve and learn its native language
     - PEER records no newcomer designation; schools are classed by language of instruction
 - field: l2Support
   bullets:
     - State-language teaching runs through curriculum and MLE, not a separate withdrawal service
     - 2003 Law on Education: equal conditions for learning the state and official languages
     - 2012-20 strategy action plan aims to enhance Kyrgyz in Russian-medium school groups
     - OSCE HCNM: MLE schools support learning mother tongue and Kyrgyz together
 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - Constitution Art 10.3 protects native-language preservation, learning and development
     - 2003 Law on Education Art 6 allows instruction in another language under conditions
     - Russian, Uzbek and Tajik medium schools exist alongside Kyrgyz-medium ones
     - MLE lets minority pupils study in their mother tongues while learning the state language
 - field: bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - MLE reaches a small minority of schools, not the whole system
     - Since 2005 MLE introduced in 78 schools and 13 kindergartens (OSCE HCNM, 2018)
     - Five universities involved in MLE teacher preparation
     - Draft multilingual education roadmap for 2025-2030 presented at Bishkek forum, Oct 2024

POLICY HISTORY ROWS:
 - year: 2003
   description: Law on Education commits the state to equal conditions for learning the state and official languages and permits instruction in another language under conditions (Art. 6)
   source: https://education-profiles.org/central-and-southern-asia/kyrgyzstan/~inclusion
 - year: 2005
   description: Multilingual education introduced in Kyrgyzstan, reaching 78 schools and 13 kindergartens by 2018
   source: https://www.osce.org/hcnm/377635
 - year: 2010
   description: Constitution makes Kyrgyz the state language and Russian an official language, and recognises all ethnicities' right to preserve and develop their native language (Art. 10)
   source: https://education-profiles.org/central-and-southern-asia/kyrgyzstan/~inclusion
 - year: 2018
   description: OSCE HCNM and the Ministry of Education and Science sign a Memorandum of Co-operation on Multilingual and Multicultural Education (12 April 2018)
   source: https://www.osce.org/hcnm/377635
 - year: 2024
   description: National Forum on Multilingual Education in Bishkek (25 October) presents a draft multilingual education roadmap for 2025–2030
   source: https://www.osce.org/hcnm/579406

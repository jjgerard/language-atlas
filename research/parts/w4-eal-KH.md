DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - Domestic indigenous minorities, not migrants; category is "ethnic minority origin"
     - 2007 Education Law Art 24: Khmer is the official language of teaching and learning
     - Art 24 leaves the language for minority-origin learners to a ministerial Prakas
     - Prakas No 48 of 10 Jan 2013 governs identification of language for such learners
 - field: l2Support
   bullets:
     - Khmer is explicitly the L2 inside the multilingual education programme
     - Preschool age 5, semester 2: 20 minutes of oral Khmer every day
     - Grade 1 Khmer is 20 per cent of time and oral only; Grades 4-6 are 100 per cent Khmer
     - MEAP 2019-23 target: primary MLE teachers and contract teachers from 197 to 253
 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - Orthographies for five indigenous languages developed from 1990, approved 2003
     - MLE runs in Ratanakiri, Mondulkiri, Kratie, Stung Treng and Preah Vihear
     - Languages in use: Phnong, Tumpuon, Kavet, Kroeung, Prov, Kouy; Charai in preparation
     - Mother tongue is 80 per cent of Grade 1 time, falling to 30 per cent by Grade 3
 - field: bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - An early-exit transitional model, not maintenance bilingual education
     - Guideline No 2972 of 26 Aug 2010 set up bilingual education in the highland provinces
     - L1 share falls 80/60/30 per cent across Grades 1-3, Khmer only from Grade 4
     - Piloted 2002 as the Highland Children Education Project with CARE in Ratanakiri

   source: https://education-profiles.org/sites/default/files/2020-03/MEAP%202019-2023.pdf

   description: Education Law Art. 24 makes Khmer the official language of teaching and learning and refers the language of minority-origin learners to ministerial Prakas
   source: https://education-profiles.org/sites/default/files/2020-03/MEAP%202019-2023.pdf

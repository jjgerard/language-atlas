 - field: l2Support
   prose: |
     Not established from the sources consulted. Mongolian is the constitutional official
     language (Art. 8) and the 2002 Law on Education as amended in 2006 "permits the use of
     the local language for communicating in the school environment (Art. 30.1.12)", but no
     source retrieved in this session sets out a Mongolian-as-a-second-language provision for
     Kazakh- or Tuvan-speaking pupils: no entitlement, no hours, no designated staff.
     Searched: UNESCO PEER Mongolia inclusion profile, the Constitute Project text of the
     Constitution, and the Asia-Pacific Multilingual Education Working Group Mongolia country
     profile at asiapacificmle.net, which returned HTTP 200 but served an empty JavaScript
     shell with no retrievable text. Not asserted either way.

 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - Entitlement covers pre-primary and primary only, per the PEER profile
     - Constitution Art 8 protects minorities' use of native languages in learning
     - Kazakh and Tuvan children have free access to pre-primary and primary in mother tongue
     - Special higher education quotas are reserved for Tuvan students

 - field: bilingualEducationNotes
   prose: |
     Not established from the sources consulted. The PEER Mongolia inclusion profile records a
     mother-tongue entitlement at pre-primary and primary level and a 2017 Asian Development
     Bank project "improving textbooks and other teaching and learning materials in the Kazakh
     language", but describes no programme that teaches content through two languages: no
     transition model, no language allocation, no named bilingual schools. Searched: PEER
     Mongolia inclusion profile, Constitute Project constitutional text, and the Asia-Pacific
     Multilingual Education Working Group country profile (JavaScript shell, no text).
     Mongolia was cited as an international example at Kyrgyzstan's 2024 National Forum on
     Multilingual Education (https://www.osce.org/hcnm/579406), but that release gives no
     description of what the Mongolian model is, so nothing is inferred from it.
     Not asserted either way.

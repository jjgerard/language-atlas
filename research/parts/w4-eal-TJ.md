DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - Domestic minority-medium schooling, not migration, is the question here
     - 1994 Constitution Art 2: Tajik is the state language, all groups may use their mother tongue
     - Medium of instruction set by a required minimum number of pupils, classes and groups
     - No newcomer or additional-language designation appears in the PEER profile
 - field: l2Support
   bullets:
     - Support is structural, through choice of medium, not a designated language service
     - Law on Education: state language is the main medium in educational institutions
     - Citizens may choose their language of instruction alongside study of the state language
     - OSCE HCNM works on balancing minority-language learning with state-language proficiency
 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - Native-language medium is conditional on the system being able to meet the need
     - Law allows native-language schooling in areas with a high concentration of ethnic groups
     - 224 of 3,836 general schools teach in Uzbek, 36 in Kyrgyz, 26 in Russian
     - Education strategy to 2020 planned minority-language textbooks and teacher training
 - field: bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Bilingual schools are a long-standing structural feature, not a designed programme
     - 620 schools teach in Tajik and Uzbek, 22 in Tajik and Kyrgyz, 22 in three languages
     - OSCE Central Asia Education Programme has supported minority-school MLE since 2012
     - 2019 Memorandum of Understanding signed with the Tajikistan Education Ministry

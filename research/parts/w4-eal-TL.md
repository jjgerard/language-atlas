DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: newcomerCriteria
   bullets:
     - No newcomer designation; the question is framed as language of instruction, not migration
     - Two official languages, Tetum and Portuguese, are both L2 for most rural children
     - Over 20 languages spoken nationally, per the 2016 EMBLI endline assessment
     - UNESCO PEER: many students do not speak either official language on entry
 - field: l2Support
   bullets:
     - No separate second-language service; support is the medium-of-instruction rule itself
     - Standard schools use both official languages as media of instruction
     - National Base Curriculum: Tetum as medium in early basic, planned Portuguese literacy
     - April 2015 ministerial rules regulate school language use, per 2017 Inclusive Ed Policy
 - field: l1Support
   bullets:
     - Provision is a pilot, not a national entitlement
     - EMBLI mother-tongue pilot launched 2012, teaching from 2013
     - Fataluku (Lautem), Galolen (Manatuto), Baikeno (Oe-cusse) used as media of instruction
     - 2011-30 NESP commits to mother tongue use in the early years
 - field: bilingualEducationNotes
   bullets:
     - Evidence is the 2016 EMBLI endline evaluation, not a national programme
     - EMBLI ran in two preschools and two primary schools per pilot district
     - Covers last preschool year, Grade 1 and Grade 2
     - Tetum writing from last trimester of Grade 2; Portuguese oral only by end of Grade 2

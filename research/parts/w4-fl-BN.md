STATUS: documented

THE ENGLISH QUESTION, DECIDED FROM THE SOURCE: SPN21 does NOT treat English as a foreign language.
It lists "English Language" as a CORE SUBJECT from Year 1, and in the very same tables it gives
English as the MEDIUM OF INSTRUCTION for Mathematics and Science from Year 1 onwards. The document's
own historical section says the medium for Mathematics in Years 1-3 "has been changed to English
since 2008", and that at upper primary "all subjects were taught in English except Bahasa Melayu,
Islamic Religious Knowledge, Physical Education, Art and History". So for this map, Brunei's answer
to "is a language other than the school language compulsory" is complicated by the fact that English
IS one of the two school languages under the dwibahasa (bilingual) policy of 1985. The languages that
are unambiguously additional in SPN21 are Arabic, French and Mandarin, which are OPTIONAL and start
at Year 7.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - SPN21 treats English as a school language, not a foreign one, under the 1985 dwibahasa policy
     - English Language is a core subject from Year 1, alongside Bahasa Melayu
     - It is also the medium for Mathematics and Science from Year 1, Maths since 2008
     - No other additional language is compulsory or available before Year 7
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - English Language stays a core subject through Year 10/11 and is taken at 'O' Level
     - Arabic, French and Mandarin remain optional on both the general and applied tracks
     - At Years 7-8 a learner may pick only one optional subject in total, languages included
     - Optional languages are examined externally by Cambridge 'O' Level or IGCSE
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Optional languages from Year 7: Arabic, French and Mandarin, each taught in itself
     - At Years 9-11 the list adds Literature in English, Kesusasteraan Melayu and "Other languages"
     - French is listed for IGCSE only; Arabic and Mandarin for both 'O' Level and IGCSE
     - "Other languages" is an open row in the table with no language named

POLICY HISTORY ROWS:
 - year: 1984
   description: Bilingual Education Policy formulated, merging the Malay-medium and English-medium school systems
 - year: 1985
   description: Bilingual (dwibahasa) Education Policy implemented, giving all government schools one national curriculum
 - year: 1992
   description: Bilingual Education Policy extended to private schools, international schools excepted
 - year: 2008
   description: Medium of instruction for Mathematics in Primary 1-3 changed to English
 - year: 2013
   description: Revised SPN21 framework published, setting core, general and optional subjects with their media of instruction

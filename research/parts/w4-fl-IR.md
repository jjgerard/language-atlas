### IR|Iran
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: Arabic is NOT a foreign language in the Iranian national curriculum. It has its own learning
area alongside the Qur'an and the document calls it "زبان دین", the language of religion. The foreign
languages in Iran's own categorisation are English, French, German "and other languages approved by the
Supreme Council of Education".

TEXT-EXTRACTION CAVEAT: the PDF's embedded Persian loses the lam-alef ligature, so words such as اسلامی
come out of the extractor as اسالمی. Quotes below are reproduced exactly as extracted; a reader checking
them against the printed page should expect that one systematic substitution.

EVIDENCE:
 - field: primaryRequirement — the decisive sentence
   quote: "آموزش زبان های خارجی از ابتدای دوره‌ متوسطه اول آغاز می‌شود و هدف آن آموزش چهار مهارت زبانی و آشناسازی دانش آموزان با مهارت‌های ارتباطی در چارچوب جهت‌گیری‌های کلی مورد نظر خواهد بود."
   source: the PDF above, section 10, حوزه‌ تربیت و یادگیری زبان‌های خارجی
   [Translation for the record: the teaching of foreign languages BEGINS AT THE START OF LOWER SECONDARY
   (دوره متوسطه اول), and its aim is the four language skills and familiarising pupils with communicative
   skills. So no foreign language is required anywhere in Iranian primary school under this document.]
 - field: primaryRequirement — the status the curriculum gives it
   quote: "ــ به کارگیری مهارت های یک زبان خارجی در چارچوب بخش انتخابی (نیمه تجویزی) برنامه درسی، با رعایت اصل تثبیت و تقویت هویت اسالمی ــ ایرانی"
   source: same, list of graduate competencies (عمل)
   [Translation: using the skills of ONE foreign language within the SELECTIVE (semi-prescriptive) part of
   the curriculum, observing the principle of establishing and strengthening Islamic-Iranian identity.
   This is important and easy to miss: the national curriculum places foreign language in the selective,
   "semi-prescriptive" portion, and attaches an identity condition to it.]
 - field: primaryRequirement — Arabic is filed elsewhere, as religion
   quote: "ــ به کارگیری مهارت های زبان و ادبیات فارسی و زبان عربی به عنوان زبان دین؛"
   source: same (the competency immediately preceding the foreign-language one)
   [Persian language and literature and Arabic "as the language of religion" are one competency; a foreign
   language is a separate one. Do not count Arabic as Iran's foreign language.]
 - field: languagesOffered — which languages
   quote: "آموزش به یکی از زبان های انگلیسی، فرانسوی، آلمانی و یا سایر زبان‌هایی که شورای عالی آموزش و پرورش تصویب کند ارائه خواهد شد."
   source: same
   [Translation: teaching will be provided in ONE of English, French, German, or other languages that the
   Supreme Council of Education approves. Note "یکی از" — one of them, not several.]
 - field: languagesOffered — the orientation imposed on the subject
   quote: "آموزش زبان‌های خارجی باید از دائره‌ تنگِ نظریه‌ها، رویکردها و روش‌های تدوین شده در جهان فراتر رود و به عنوان بستری برای تقویت فرهنگ ملی و باورها و ارزش‌های خودی در نظر گرفته ‌شود. رویکرد آموزش زبان‌های خارجی، رویکرد ارتباطی فعال و خودباورانه است."
   source: same
   [Translation: foreign-language teaching must go beyond the narrow circle of theories, approaches and
   methods formulated in the world, and be treated as a ground for strengthening national culture and
   indigenous beliefs and values; the approach is an active, self-confident communicative one. The content
   at beginner levels is to be built round local topics.]
 - field: upperSecondary — what is expected by the end of it
   quote: "در دوره متوسطه دوم، دانش آموزان باید بتوانند متن‌هایی در حد متوسط را بخوانند و مفاهیم آن‌ها را دریابند. در ضمن توانایی نوشتن، در حد یک مقاله‌ کوتاه، به زبان خارجی نیز در آن‌ها تقویت شود و از توانائی‌های الزم برای استفاده از منابع در حد متوسط و برقراری ارتباط به یکی از زبان‌های خارجی را داشته باشند."
   source: same
 - field: upperSecondary — the terminal standard
   quote: "در پایان دوره‌ متوسطه دوم دانش آموزان باید توانایی خواندن و درک متون ساده‌ تخصصی و نوشتن مقاله راکسب کنند. در دوره متوسطه دوم گسترش دامنه‌ واژگان رشته‌های تخصصی به درک بهتر متون و برقراری ارتباط علمی کمک می‌کند."
   source: same
   [So the language continues through upper secondary (متوسطه دوم) and shifts towards subject-specific
   vocabulary matched to the pupil's academic track.]
 - field: languagesOffered — SOURCED NEGATIVE on the primary years
   quote: "آموزش زبان های خارجی از ابتدای دوره‌ متوسطه اول آغاز می‌شود"
   source: same
   [I read the whole foreign-languages learning area (section 10) and every occurrence of خارجی and
   انگلیسی in the 68-page document. There is NO provision for foreign language in the primary years, and
   no clause allowing schools to start earlier.]

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Arabic is not counted as foreign here: the curriculum calls it the language of religion
     - National Curriculum: foreign language teaching "begins at the start of lower secondary"
     - No foreign language is provided for in any Iranian primary grade
     - It sits in the "selective (semi-prescriptive)" part of the curriculum, not the prescribed core
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Continues into متوسطه دوم, upper secondary, with a stated terminal standard
     - Pupils are to read intermediate texts and write a short article in the language
     - By the end they should read simple specialist texts and write an essay
     - Upper-secondary work widens vocabulary towards the pupil's own academic track
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Curriculum names three: English, French and German, plus any the Supreme Council approves
     - A pupil takes ONE of them, not a combination: the text says "one of the languages"
     - Teaching must "go beyond the narrow circle" of world methods and reinforce national culture
     - The identity condition is written into the graduate competency itself

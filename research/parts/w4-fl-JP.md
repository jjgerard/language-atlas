DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Compulsory: 外国語活動 in grades 3-4, then 外国語 as a graded subject in grades 5-6
     - Statutory load 35 hours a year in grades 3-4, 70 in grades 5-6, one hour is 45 minutes
     - Grades 1 and 2 carry no foreign-language allocation in 別表第一
     - Course of Study, grades 5-6: "In principle, take English in foreign languages period"
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Continues unbroken: 外国語 is 140 hours a year in each of grades 7, 8 and 9
     - Upper secondary: 英語コミュニケーションⅠ is the only 必履修 language subject, 3 credits
     - Schools may cut it to 2 credits; the other five 外国語 subjects are elective
     - MEXT: "In principle, have students take English in Foreign Languages lessons"
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Figures are a MEXT survey of upper secondary in 2023-24, not a curriculum entitlement
     - Course of Study permits "other foreign languages" on the English objectives
     - Only 603 upper-secondary schools offer any non-English language, over 16 languages
     - Chinese 449 schools, Korean 344, French 188, Spanish 107, German 98; Thai 1, Mongolian 1

POLICY HISTORY ROWS:
 - year: 2017
   description: Revised Course of Study makes 外国語活動 compulsory in grades 3-4 and 外国語 a graded subject in grades 5-6
 - year: 2018
   description: Revised upper-secondary Course of Study makes 英語コミュニケーションⅠ the sole compulsory foreign-language subject, 3 credits
 - year: 2025
   description: MEXT reports 603 upper-secondary schools teaching a language other than English, across 16 languages

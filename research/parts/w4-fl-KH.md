DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Foreign Languages is a timetabled subject from Grade 1, 2 hours a week in every primary grade
     - One study hour is 40 minutes in primary, on a six-day week of 30 hours
     - Computer and Local Life Skills start only at Grade 4; foreign language does not wait
     - Curriculum names it a core competency: "English and/or French"
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Continues and doubles: 6 hours a week in Grades 7-9 and in every year of Grades 10-12
     - Split as 4 hours for Living Language 1 and 2 hours for Living Language 2
     - Identical 6 hours on both the Science and the Social Science upper-secondary strands
     - Only compulsory subject whose weight does not shift between the two strands
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Only two languages exist in the framework: English and French, with no third permitted
     - Both are compulsory: whichever is not taken first becomes Living Language 2
     - Pupil's primary choice fixes the order, English first then French, or the reverse
     - Framework gives no leave for a school to substitute another language

EVIDENCE:
 - field: primaryRequirement — the statutory aim, as quoted by the ETH factbook from the Law on Education
   quote: "artistic culture, to learn foreign langue from the third years of elementary education upward.' The secondary education subsequently has the aim to '(…) provide general knowledge and necessary basic knowledge for continuing education and engaging in suitable professions' (Lao PDR, 2007a, S. Article 18)"
   source: https://ethz.ch/.../X_CES%20Factbook%20Education%20Systems_Laos_2022_AlexBenhauresch_final.pdf
   [The factbook is quoting the Lao Law on Education (as amended), Article 18. The typo "langue" is in the
   factbook's own text. I did NOT retrieve the Law on Education itself, so this is a secondary quotation of
   a statute — flag it as such if published.]
 - field: primaryRequirement — the subject list and the start grade
   quote: "Students at primary school receive education in nine subjects. These include Lao Language, mathematics, handicraft, moral education, world around us, physical education, art, music, and English, which is introduced in Grade 3."
   source: same
 - field: primaryRequirement — that a compulsory rule exists
   quote: "The education law, which was revised 2015, defines lower secondary education as compulsory. Additionally, it states the English language as a compulsory subject from Grade three onwards of primary education (Noonan, 2020)."
   source: same
 - field: primaryRequirement — the ministry's own confirmation that the Grade 3 English curriculum is new
   quote: "In the new Grade 3 English primary curriculum developed by the Ministry of Education and Sports (MoES), with support from Australia through the BEQUAL program, and being launched this school year, students will learn to read and write English using a phonics approach."
   source: https://bequal-laos.org/2021/09/30/...
   [Dated 30 September 2021, so "this school year" is 2021-22.]
 - field: languagesOffered — the practice gap, in the programme's own words
   quote: "But learning to pronounce the sounds in English will be a new skill for most teachers in Laos. Without any modelling, teachers won't know how to pronounce the sounds correctly and thus won't be able to teach them."
   source: same
   [This is the paper-versus-practice finding for Laos, and it comes from the ministry's own delivery
   partner, not from an outside critic.]
 - field: languagesOffered — French enters at lower secondary
   quote: "The lower secondary curriculum comprises additional subjects. These include biology, chemistry, physics, history, geography, civics, French, information and communication technology, basic vocational training, sports education, classroom and school activities, extracurricular activities, vocational guidance, and national security education."
   source: https://ethz.ch/.../X_CES%20Factbook%20Education%20Systems_Laos_2022_AlexBenhauresch_final.pdf
 - field: languagesOffered — the ministry names foreign languages as a resource problem
   quote: "The key challenge is additional finance for infrastructure, high quality staff and specialist materials to teach mathematics, science subjects, IT and foreign languages as well as to provide reading rooms/libraries for student and teacher research."
   source: https://rtm.org.la/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ESSDP-Final-Eng-Version-update-30-Oct-2020.pdf (upper secondary chapter)
 - field: primaryRequirement — still an aspiration in the 2021-25 plan
   quote: "Strategy 2: Improve and revise primary curriculum and ensure the contents are appropriate and inclusive for the country. Key activities: • Revise grade 3 – grade 5 curriculum; • Promote teaching foreign language in primary schools;"
   source: same
   [Note the tension worth recording: the sector plan for 2021-25 still lists "promote teaching foreign
   language in primary schools" as an activity to be undertaken, alongside a concern that Grades 1-2 have
   too many subjects for Lao language and mathematics.]
 - field: upperSecondary — what the ministry prioritises there
   quote: "Intermediate Outcome 4: Upper secondary in-service teacher training programs prioritise teachers of natural sciences, Lao language, mathematics and English language"
   source: same
   [English is one of four upper-secondary training priorities, which evidences that it is taught at upper
   secondary — but the plan states no rule about whether it is compulsory there.]

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Start grade from a secondary quotation of the Law on Education, not from the statute itself
     - English is compulsory from Grade 3 of primary, one of nine primary subjects
     - Nothing is required in Grades 1 and 2, where the plan worries about subject overload
     - A new Grade 3 English curriculum, phonics-based, launched in the 2021-22 school year
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Not established from the sources consulted: no rule on whether English continues compulsorily
     - English is one of four upper-secondary in-service training priorities in the 2021-25 plan
     - Ministry names staff and materials for foreign languages as its key upper-secondary challenge
     - No leaving or admission language requirement was found in any retrieved document
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Two languages appear: English from Grade 3, French added at lower secondary
     - MoES delivery partner in 2021: pronouncing English sounds is "a new skill for most teachers"
     - Sector plan 2021-25 still lists promoting foreign language in primary as work to do
     - No third foreign language appears in any retrieved document

### MC|Monaco
STATUS: documented (fields requested: upperSecondary, languagesOffered)

THE QUESTION THE TASK ASKED: does Monaco's upperSecondary genuinely differ from France's? ANSWER FROM
THE SOURCE: not in hours, programmes or diplomas — the Principality's schools are accredited as
"établissement français à l'étranger", and the booklet says in terms that timetables, syllabuses and
diplomas conform to those set by the French education ministry, with examinations attached to the
Académie de Nice. What IS Monegasque is the SHAPE of the language offer around that French core:
English from the first year of nursery school, Monegasque as a taught subject, and a lycée-level
choice of European sections in English or Italian plus an American-section BFI.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Not a separate system: schools are accredited "établissement français à l'étranger"
     - Hours, syllabuses and diplomas follow the French ministry's; exams via Académie de Nice
     - Lycée Albert-Ier adds a BFI American section and a European section in English
     - Lycée Rainier-III offers a European section in English or Italian, tied to the vocational track
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - English is taught from the first year of école maternelle, by specialist teachers in primary
     - Collège Charles-III: LV1bis in 6e from German, Spanish, Italian, Chinese or Russian
     - Private collège FANB lists German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, no Chinese
     - Monegasque is taught CE2 to 3e; French is also offered as FLE to non-francophone pupils

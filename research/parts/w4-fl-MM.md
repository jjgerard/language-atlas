### MM|Myanmar
STATUS: documented (with a sourcing caveat on the host — read the SOURCES note)

FRAMING: the Myanmar National Curriculum Framework NEVER uses the word "foreign" — the term does not
occur once in the 19-page document (term count on the extracted text: "foreign" = 0). English is treated
as one of the compulsory learning areas and as a permitted MEDIUM OF INSTRUCTION alongside Myanmar.
Ethnic languages are a third category again, handled under Local Curriculum and by State/Region
governments. Record Myanmar's categories, not ours.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Framework never says "foreign": English is a core learning area and a permitted medium
     - "English shall be taught starting from the primary level", i.e. from Grade 1
     - 3 periods a week, 72 hours a year, in Grades 1-3; 5 periods, 120 hours, in Grades 4-5
     - A lower-primary period is 40 minutes in a 36-week year
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Continues as compulsory throughout: 6 periods a week, 162 hours a year, at middle school
     - High school: one of six common core areas, on the Science and the Arts stream alike
     - 5 periods a week in Grades 10 and 11, rising to 6 periods in Grade 12
     - No option to drop it: English is core on both streams in every high-school grade
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Only English is named; no French, Chinese or other language is in the national structure
     - Myanmar or English or both may be the medium of instruction under Education Law s.43
     - Ethnic languages are devolved to State and Region governments under s.44
     - They sit in Local Curriculum, capped and shared with local history, farming and computing

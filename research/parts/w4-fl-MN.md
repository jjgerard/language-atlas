DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Starting grade not established: it is set below statute, in a curriculum concept
     - General Law on Education 2023 art. 6.1: the main foreign language of instruction is English
     - Same clause preserves the right to study in one's mother tongue and other foreign languages
     - Statute names the language but attaches no grade, age or compulsion to it
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - No leaving or admission language requirement found in either 2023 education law
     - Government target: 1,800-plus grade-12 leavers a year to sit TOEFL Junior or Duolingo
     - The state pays half that test fee under Government Resolution 372 of 2021
     - Separate target of 50-plus schools teaching maths and science through English
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - English is the only language named in either 2023 education law
     - "Other foreign languages" survive as a preserved right, with no list and no entitlement
     - Schools with a permit may teach through a foreign language altogether
     - Mongolian language, script, literature and history must still be taught in Mongolian

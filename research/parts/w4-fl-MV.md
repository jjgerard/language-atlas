### MV|Maldives
STATUS: documented

IMPORTANT FRAMING: English is NOT the foreign language in the Maldives. The National Curriculum
Framework makes English the MEDIUM OF INSTRUCTION from primary onwards; the first language is Dhivehi.
The languages the framework itself calls foreign are Arabic, French and Mandarin.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - English is the medium of instruction from primary on, so it is not the foreign language here
     - NCF says pupils "get access to Modern Foreign Language(s) beginning from Key Stage 1", grade 1
     - Its own Table 3 shows only one optional language at Key Stages 1 to 3, namely Arabic
     - Access is an option, not a requirement: Arabic sits in the "Optional subject" column
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Named foreign languages appear only at Key Stage 4, grades 9-10, as one elective among many
     - Key Stage 4 pupils choose any 4 electives from at least two learning areas
     - At Key Stage 5, grades 11-12, the only core language subjects are Islam and Dhivehi
     - No foreign language is listed in the Key Stage 5 elective column; even English is elective there
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Table 3 is marked "DRAFT, 09 Apr 2014" inside the published framework
     - Grades 1 to 8 offer one optional language only: Arabic
     - Grades 9-10 name three: "Foreign Lang (Arabic, French, Mandarin)"
     - Arabic is also a medium of instruction in specialist schools, not only a taught language

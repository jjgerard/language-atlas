### NG|Nigeria
STATUS: documented (fields requested: upperSecondary, languagesOffered)

FRAMING: in Nigeria the foreign languages are FRENCH and ARABIC. English is the school language and is
core throughout; "Nigerian Languages" are indigenous languages, not foreign ones, and should not be
counted in this field — though what happens to them at senior secondary is recorded below because the
new curriculum moves them.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Neither French nor Arabic is among the five compulsory senior secondary subjects
     - Core is English, General Mathematics, one Trade subject, Citizenship and Heritage, Digital Tech
     - French and Arabic drop to Humanities electives, alongside History, Government, CRS and Music
     - A Nigerian language is also only an elective at senior secondary, not core as in JSS
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Only two foreign languages appear in the national offering: French and Arabic
     - Arabic is optional from Primary 1; French is optional only from Primary 4
     - Both stay "(Optional)" at every level, above a compulsory minimum of 9 to 12 subjects
     - At Primary 4-6 the compulsory load is 11 subjects and French is subject 12

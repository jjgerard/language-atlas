DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Foreign Languages is one of eight compulsory learning areas, running from Grade 1
     - Curriculum: the prescribed language for the whole core curriculum "is English"
     - Time: 40 hours a year in Grades 1-3, rising to 80 a year in Grades 4-6
     - Grade-level indicators apply to every year of compulsory schooling, Grades 1-9
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Continues as a basic learning area at Grades 10-12, worth 6 of the 41 basic credits
     - 240 hours across the three upper-secondary years, against 120 a year in Grades 7-9
     - Indicators are set for the whole Grade 10-12 interval, not grade by grade
     - Schools may add further courses "depending on their readiness and priorities"
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - English is the only language the national core curriculum prescribes
     - Others named as examples: French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Pali
     - Curriculum also names "languages of neighbouring countries" without listing them
     - All non-English languages are left "to the discretion of educational institutions"

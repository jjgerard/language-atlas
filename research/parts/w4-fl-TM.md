 - field: primaryRequirement — the statutory rule, and it is unambiguous
   quote: "Daşary ýurt dilleriniň öwrenilmegi umumybilim we hünär bilimleriniň maksatnamalaryna hökmany dersler hökmünde girizilýär."
   source: https://bilim.gov.tm/media/legal_documents/document_5.pdf (Law on Education, Article 5, paragraph 4)
   [Translation for the record: the study of foreign languages is entered into general-education and
   vocational-education programmes AS COMPULSORY SUBJECTS. The clause states compulsion but names no grade,
   no age and no language.]

   source: same
   [Translation: taking account of growing global social, ecological and economic interdependence, the
   State assists citizens of Turkmenistan to study foreign languages in all types of educational

   That is the nearest the statute comes to naming languages, and it names none directly.]

   [Translation: schools organise the teaching of foreign languages to pupils IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE

 - field: upperSecondary — the higher-education bridge
   quote: "Ýokary hünär bilimi edarasyna okuwa kabul edilen talyplar bilen okuw işini geçirmek üçin, olaryň zerur bolan döwlet dilini ýa-da degişli daşary ýurt dilini bilşini ýeterlik derejede kämilleşdirmek maksady bilen, bir okuw ýylynyň dowamynda dil taýýarlygy boýunça okuwlar guralyp bilner."
   source: https://bilim.gov.tm/media/legal_documents/document_5.pdf (Article 5, paragraph 6)
   [Translation: a one-academic-year language preparation course may be organised for students admitted to
   a higher vocational education institution, to bring their command of the state language or of the
   relevant foreign language up to the necessary level, and passing it lets them continue their programme.
   This is a remedial provision at the point of entry, not a school-leaving language requirement.]
 - field: languagesOffered — the standing policy instrument
   quote: "Teaching is carried out within the framework of the 'Concept for improving the teaching of foreign languages in Turkmenistan', from the date of adoption of which in December this year marks 5 years. The concept includes three stages and is currently at the stage of the third. At its first stage, in 2018, the world experience in the field of methodological support was actively mastered, state standards, training programs and curricula were improved at all levels of the educational process. At the second stage (2019-2020), the system of training and advanced training of teachers, the educational and methodological base in this area as a whole, were improved. The goal of the third, final stage (2021-2024) is the need to create a continuous, consistent and interconnected system of teaching foreign languages in Turkmenistan."
   source: https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/en/post/66811/foreign-languages-are-important-priority-education (5 October 2022)
   [Note what the third stage's stated goal implies: as of 2022 the state's own account is that a
   "continuous, consistent and interconnected" system of foreign-language teaching did not yet exist and
   was the object of the 2021-2024 stage. That is the paper-versus-practice point for Turkmenistan, in the
   state news agency's own words.]

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Starting grade not established: the law sets compulsion but leaves the grade to curriculum plans
     - Law on Education art. 5: foreign languages enter school programmes "as compulsory subjects"
     - Turkmen is the main language of teaching in every type of institution
     - Schools must organise foreign-language teaching "in accordance with the curriculum plans"
 - field: upperSecondary
   bullets:
     - Not established from the sources consulted: no rule found on the upper-secondary stage as such
     - The compulsory-subject rule in art. 5 covers general and vocational programmes alike
     - No school-leaving language requirement appears in the Law on Education
     - Universities may run a one-year language preparation course for admitted students
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Statute names no language; it points only to "the official working languages of the UN"
     - A foreign language may itself be the main medium in some institutions
     - Where it is, the state language still carries the national part of the programme
     - State media 2022: a "continuous, consistent" language system was still the 2021-24 goal

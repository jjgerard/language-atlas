TREATY STATUS ACTUALLY OBSERVED (correction to the task brief):
 - ECRML (ETS 148): the Albania row on the chart is EMPTY in both the Signature and the Ratification columns. Albania has neither signed nor ratified the Charter. The brief's statement that Albania "signed but did not ratify" is wrong. No Committee of Experts report exists for Albania.
 - FCNM (ETS 157): Albania — Signature 29/06/1995, Ratification 28/09/1999, Entry into force 01/01/2000. Albania is a party, and the Fifth Advisory Committee Opinion is the substantive education source used here.

POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1999
   description: "Albania ratifies the Framework Convention for the Protection of National Minorities on 28 September 1999 (in force 1 January 2000); Albania has neither signed nor ratified the European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages"
 - year: 2017
   description: "Ligj nr. 96/2017, datë 13.10.2017 'Për mbrojtjen e pakicave kombëtare', Neni 13, recognises a right to learn a minority language and, where a minority lives traditionally or in substantial number and there is sufficient demand, to receive lessons in it, leaving all three thresholds to a Council of Ministers decision; names nine national minorities including Greek and Macedonian"
 - year: 2018
   description: "Council of Ministers Decision no. 561 of 29 September 2018 enables local government to take the initiative to open classes in national minority languages (as described in the Fifth FCNM Opinion on Albania, para. 146)"
 - year: 2020
   description: "Council of Ministers Decision no. 1155 of 24 December 2020 defines 'substantial numbers' and 'sufficient demand', imposing a 20% minority share of the administrative unit, proof of residency against the 2010 civil registry, a written parental request and a minimum of 15 pupils per class"
 - year: 2022
   description: "An amended instruction of the Albanian Ministry of Education and Sports, from August 2022, allows national minority language education in grades 1-9 regardless of the number of students, disapplying the 15-pupil class threshold"
 - year: 2023
   description: "Advisory Committee's Fifth Opinion on Albania, adopted 6 June 2023, finds minority language education still confined to the same few municipalities and urges the 20% threshold be reviewed or lowered"

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Read on the LEX.AO reproduction of Lei 17/16, not the Diario da Republica text
     - Lei 17/16 Art 16(1): teaching must be delivered in Portuguese
     - Art 16(3) allows Angolan languages as a complement and learning instrument
     - Their use is left to a separate regulating instrument the Act does not issue
     - No transition grade arises, since Portuguese is the medium from the start
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - "Not established from the sources consulted. Lei n.º 17/16 treats Angolan languages only as a complement and learning instrument alongside Portuguese-medium teaching (Art. 16) and names their valorisation among the aims of the adult-education subsystem (Art. 53); it creates no timetabled national-language subject in general education. The UNESCO PEER inclusion profile for Angola carries no ethnic-and-linguistic-groups section. Searched: full text of Lei 17/16, PEER Angola inclusion profile."

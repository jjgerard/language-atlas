NOT-ESTABLISHED NOTE:
 - Which ethnic languages are actually timetabled, in which years, and whether any pupil is entitled to them, was not established from the sources consulted. Neither education law names a minority language as a subject; the only instrument found is the 1992 Decree, reported by UNESCO PEER, which was not retrieved in its own text in this session.

   bullets:

     - Art 7.2: other languages only by international treaty or executive-authority agreement
     - General Education Law Art 6.2: such teaching needs accredited standards or programmes
     - UNESCO: secondary provision is in Azerbaijani, Russian, Georgian and Armenian

   bullets:

     - 1992 Decree said to provide conditions for teaching ethnic languages at secondary schools

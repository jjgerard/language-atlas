ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): Ley 070 art. 7 sets the L1/L2 allocation by
community language predominance, not by parental request and not by a minimum-enrolment
threshold. No enrolment trigger, opt-in or opt-out appears anywhere in art. 7.

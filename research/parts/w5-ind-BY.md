ABSENCE FOUND AND STATED:
 - Article 82, read in full, sets NO pupil-number threshold, NO territorial trigger and NO parental-petition procedure for obtaining Belarusian-medium instruction. The only qualifier on the guaranteed choice is the founder's discretion and the phrase "при наличии такой возможности".
 - The Code nowhere requires any named subject to be taught in Belarusian. The word "белорусском"/"белорусского" appears in Article 82 only in the sense of the network of Belarusian-medium provision (82.2) and of Belarusian as a compulsory language subject (82.3, 82.5).
 - Belarusian is not treated by the Code as a "язык национального меньшинства"; that category (82.6) is a separate, permissive route for other languages.

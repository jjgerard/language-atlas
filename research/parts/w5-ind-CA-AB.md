ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): the Education Act s. 17 names no Indigenous
language; it gives a student an entitlement to English only, and everything beyond that is a
board authorisation. There is no minimum-enrolment or petition right for an Indigenous
language anywhere in ss. 17 or 19; s. 19(2) is a parental request that the board may grant if
it judges it has the resources. Blackfoot and Cree have provincial Language and Culture
programmes (the subject) but do not appear in the list of provincially available Language
Arts programmes, which the same Guide to Education page identifies as the component of a
bilingual programme.

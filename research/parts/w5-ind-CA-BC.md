DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - School Act s 5(1): every student is entitled to a programme provided in English
     - s 5(2) entitlement to another medium runs only to Charter s 23 rights holders
     - s 5(3): a board may provide a programme in another language, minister approving
     - So Indigenous-language immersion is permissive, board-led, and needs ministerial sign-off
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Second language compulsory Grades 5 to 8; an Indigenous language can satisfy it
     - 21 Indigenous language Integrated Resource Packages are specified programme guides
     - Only languages with a Grade 5 to 8 provincial curriculum count for the requirement
     - Local language without an IRP needs a board agreement with a band or treaty nation

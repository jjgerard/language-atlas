ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): s.79 of The Public Schools Act creates a
parental petition entitlement (23 pupils) only for English or French as the language
of instruction. There is no equivalent petition right for an Indigenous language, and
no minimum-enrolment trigger for one anywhere in s.79.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Public Schools Act s 79(1): English and French are the languages of instruction
     - s 79(2)(e): another language may be a medium for up to 50% of regular school hours
     - That use needs school board authorisation and is capped by the minister
     - The 23-pupil parental petition right in s 79(3) covers English and French only
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - s 79(2)(b): another language may be used in a period the minister authorises for it
     - 2007 K-12 Aboriginal Languages and Cultures framework is the basis for curricula
     - Framework is generic; specific language curricula are developed from it locally
     - Senior Years topics become school-initiated courses or student-initiated projects

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Education Act s 70(1): medium of instruction must be an Official Language
     - Nine of the eleven official languages are Indigenous, from Chipewyan to Tłı̨chǫ
     - District Education Authority picks it, subject to demand, fluent teachers, materials
     - Where an Indigenous language is the medium, English must be taught as a subject
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Where English is the medium, s 73(3) requires another Official Language be taught
     - District Education Authority sets which language, following Ministerial directions
     - ILE Policy: schools teaching a language must give it dedicated timetabled time
     - Our Languages curriculum runs five levels, Emergent to Capable by end of Grade 12

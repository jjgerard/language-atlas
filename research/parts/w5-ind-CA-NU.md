### CA|Nunavut
STATUS: documented

NOTE ON NAME: the statute is now the **Inuktut Protection Act**, C.S.Nu., c.I-140.
The Legislative Assembly's own consolidation notice confirms a "September 18, 2025
consolidation of the Inuktut Protection Act" reflecting "amendments that came into
force on September 18, 2025". The full text I could retrieve and read is the
July 1, 2021 official consolidation, still titled *Inuit Language Protection Act*;
the amendments recorded in the 2025 notice are to headings and French wording, not
to the substance of s.8. I could NOT retrieve a full text of the post-2025
consolidation — nunavutlegislation.ca returned 404 on every consolidated-law path I
tried and CanLII 403s.

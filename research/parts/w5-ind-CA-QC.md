ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): the Régime pédagogique (RLRQ c. I-13.3, r. 8) was
searched for "autochtone", "crie", "inuit" and "naskapi": zero hits. The compulsory-subject
tables for primary and for both secondary cycles name a second language that is "français ou
anglais" and nothing else. There is no Quebec provincial programme of study in any
Indigenous language, and no Indigenous language appears anywhere in the compulsory or option
tables. The only route in the regulation is art. 23.1's "programmes d'études locaux" offered
as optional subjects, which the school itself designs.

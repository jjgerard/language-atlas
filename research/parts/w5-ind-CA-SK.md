ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): s. 180 of The Education Act, 1995 was read in full.
It names only English and French. No Indigenous language is named anywhere in it, and there is
no minimum-enrolment or parental-petition trigger of any kind for an Indigenous language as a
medium. The Registrar's Handbook's eight Required Areas of Study do not include a second
language at all; a second language, Indigenous or otherwise, is a locally determined option
decided by the board of education. Searching the Registrar's Handbook for "Cree", "Dene",
"Michif", "Nakawe", "Nakoda", "Dakota" and "Indigenous language" returned no hits.

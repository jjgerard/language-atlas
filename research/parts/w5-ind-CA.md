### CA|Canada
STATUS: documented

FRAMING: the federal entry records what CANADA as a national jurisdiction does.
Schooling itself is provincial/territorial or First Nations-run; the federal
instrument recognises language rights and funds programmes but creates no
schooling entitlement. That is a real finding, not an absence.

ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): the Indigenous Languages Act contains no
provision creating a right to be taught in, or to be taught, an Indigenous language
in school. Sections 8 and 9 are enabling (the Minister "may … enter into agreements")
and both are expressly subject to "the powers and jurisdictions … of the provinces
and territories".

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - No federal medium-of-instruction rule: schooling is provincial or First Nations-run
     - Indigenous Languages Act 2019 s 6 recognises s 35 rights include language rights
     - s 8 lets the Minister agree language programmes "in relation to education"
     - ISC funds on-reserve schools by formula, including language and culture programming
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - The 2019 Act creates no entitlement to be taught an Indigenous language at school
     - Purposes include language nest, mentorship and immersion programmes, s 5(b)(iv)
     - Treaty or self-government agreement prevails over the Act where they conflict, s 4
     - Delivery rests with provinces, territories and First Nations education authorities

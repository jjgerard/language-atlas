POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1980
   description: "Act No. 20/80 of 11 September 1980 on the reorganization of the education system: article 4 provides that the two national languages, Lingala and Munukutuba/Kituba, are taught in school"
 - year: 1990
   description: "Loi scolaire n° 008/90 du 6 septembre 1990 portant réorganisation du système éducatif en République du Congo"
 - year: 1995
   description: "Loi n° 25/95 du 17 novembre 1995 modifies the 1990 school law; the school law in force sets out no language of instruction, addressing language only in its literacy provisions"
 - year: 2002
   description: "Ministère de l'Enseignement primaire et secondaire chargé de l'alphabétisation publishes the Plan national d'action de l'éducation pour tous (Brazzaville, November 2002); its Action 9, budgeted for 2003-2015, is to introduce the national languages as languages of teaching and learning in the early primary years while keeping French, and to produce literacy materials in French, Lingala and Kituba"
 - year: 2015
   description: "Constitution of 6 November 2015, article 4: French is the official language and Lingala and Kituba are the national vehicular languages"
 - year: 2015
   description: "Stratégie sectorielle de l'éducation 2015-2025 adopted; it plans to develop ORA centres for indigenous children, which use mother tongues and national languages 'contrairement au système officiel', but names no national language anywhere else"
 - year: 2026
   description: "A projet de loi portant organisation du système éducatif, intended to replace loi n° 25-95 of 17 November 1995, is presented and adopted in the Council of Ministers on 20 January 2026 (La Nouvelle République)"

NOTES:
 - I retrieved a scanned PDF of loi n° 25-95 (https://www.unicongo.cg/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Loi-n%C2%B025-95_1995_Enseignement_Modifiant-loi-scolaire-n%C2%B0008-90_1990.pdf, http 200, 857662 bytes) but it is image-only: pdftotext returns 6 bytes and no OCR tool is available on this machine. So the statement that the school law in force contains no medium-of-instruction provision rests on Leclerc (axl, Université Laval), who reproduces its articles 49-52 on alphabétisation, not on my own reading of the statute.
 - PEER cites Act No. 20/80 of 1980 for the teaching of the two national languages. That act was superseded by loi 008/90 and then by loi 25-95, so PEER's citation is to a repealed instrument; this should not be reported as current law.
 - The La Nouvelle République account of the January 2026 bill lists preschool, primary cycle length, CEP, BTS and LMD as its innovations and says nothing about national languages. That is a press summary, not the bill text; no conclusion should be drawn about what the bill does or does not contain on language.

CORRECTION TO THE EXISTING ENTRY: the entry's `standing` field says the subject is
"Compulsory where indigenous enrolment exceeds 20%, optional elsewhere". Decreto 280 art. 5
makes it obligatory for the SCHOOL TO OFFER at that threshold; art. 4 makes the subject
optional FOR THE PUPIL AND FAMILY everywhere, by written declaration at enrolment. The
threshold controls supply, not attendance. The entry's phase-in dates also read
"first grade in 2010 to eighth by 2018"; Decreto 280 art. 2 gives 2010 for first year and
2017 for eighth. (The MINEDUC PEIB page separately says "comenzando el año 2010 ... para
alcanzar al 2018", so both figures are traceable — the decree says 2017, the portal 2018.)

ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): Ley 21.430 (2022) art. 41, the right to
education, contains no indigenous-language or intercultural-education provision; the
language right sits in art. 26 (identity) and is a general right to use one's own language,
not a schooling entitlement. Decreto 280 was retrieved in full; it creates a SUBJECT
("Sector de Aprendizaje Lengua Indígena") and nowhere makes an indigenous language a medium
for other subjects. The only medium-of-instruction instrument in play is the opt-in
bilingual regime of DS 520/1996, which Decreto 280 recites but which I did not retrieve
separately.

   bullets:
     - The Lengua Indigena subject is not a medium; it is a timetabled subject
     - DS 520/1996 lets a school opt into a bilingual regime teaching most subjects in the language
     - That regime is a school choice, not an entitlement, and is not indigenous-specific
     - Ley 19.253 art 32 tasks CONADI with an EIB system in high-density indigenous areas
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Decreto 280 art 4: optional for pupil and family, declared in writing at enrolment
     - Art 5: schools with 20% or more indigenous-descent pupils must OFFER it
     - Aymara, Mapuzugun, Quechua and Rapa Nui, four hours a week, grades 1-8
     - Taught by a pair: a classroom teacher plus a community educador tradicional

POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1990
   description: "Lei nº 103/III/90 de 29 de Dezembro approves the Bases do Sistema Educativo of Cabo Verde"
 - year: 1999
   description: "Lei nº 113/V/99 de 18 de Outubro amends the Bases do Sistema Educativo"
 - year: 2010
   description: "Decreto-Legislativo nº 2/2010 de 7 de Maio revises the Bases do Sistema Educativo; its preamble gives 'especial ênfase à política de afirmação da língua nacional cabo-verdiana, enquanto língua materna e património cultural da cabo-verdianidade', and objective (h) seeks its written affirmation 'visando sua utilização oficial a par da língua portuguesa'"
 - year: 2018
   description: "Decreto-Legislativo nº 13/2018 de 7 de dezembro makes the first amendment to Decreto-Legislativo nº 2/2010, keeping the emphasis on affirming the national Cabo Verdean language"
 - year: 2022
   description: "Decreto-lei nº 27/2022 de 12 de julho establishes the ensino básico curriculum; article 13 defines Portuguese as a non-mother tongue that is nonetheless the vehicular language and a priority curriculum area from pre-school to the end of secondary, and article 14 makes French and English compulsory from year 5"
 - year: 2022
   description: "Decreto-lei nº 28/2022 de 12 de julho establishes the ensino secundário curriculum; article 15 introduces Língua e Cultura Cabo-verdianas as an optional subject from year 10 for an experimental period of three years renewable by two, with the Cabo Verdean language as both medium and object of learning within it, and undertakes to work towards introducing the language from year 1 and bilingual education from pre-school"
 - year: 2024
   description: "Ministério da Educação publishes the Programa de Língua e Cultura Cabo-verdiana for the 11th year of schooling, recording three 50-minute weekly periods and stating that ensino básico curricula do not yet include school learning of the Cabo Verdean language"

NOTES:
 - The Decreto-Legislativo 2/2010 PDF read here is the 2010 consolidated text; I did not obtain the 2018 (DL 13/2018) consolidated version in this session. The 2018 amendment is cited by number and date inside both the 2022 Boletim Oficial and the 2024 ministry programme.
 - The LCCV programme quotes the Constitution at "n.º 2 do Artigo 9.º" and "n.º 1 do Artigo 10.º" as requiring the State to develop the Cabo Verdean language towards parity with Portuguese. I did not read the Constitution itself in this session, so that is reported as the ministry's citation, not as verified constitutional text.
 - UNESCO PEER's Cabo Verde inclusion profile has no "Language of instruction" heading and no sentence on kriolu in school; its "Ethnicity and languages" content is absent. The `~curriculum` tab 404s.

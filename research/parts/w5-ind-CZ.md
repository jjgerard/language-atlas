POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 2006
   description: "Czechia deposits its instrument of ratification of the European Charter on 15 November 2006, declaring Slovak, Polish, German and Romani as minority languages under Part II and applying Part III to Polish in Frydek-Mistek and Karvina and to Slovak nationwide"
 - year: 2023
   description: "Czechia decides to apply Part III of the Charter to German in eight districts selected from the areas where it is traditionally used"
 - year: 2024
   description: "Czechia's extended declaration takes effect on 28 February 2024, adding Article 8.1 a(ii), b(ii), c(ii), d(ii), e(i), g, h, i for German"

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Act 96/AN/00 Art 5: education and training are provided in the official and national languages
     - Afar and Somali are the national languages named in the implementing decree
     - Neither the Act nor the profile fixes years, subjects or a transition grade
     - Framed as entitlement in law rather than as a described classroom arrangement
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - A Council of Ministers decree sets methods for teaching in French, Arabic, Afar and Somali
     - The 2012 ministry-organisation act repeats those four teaching languages
     - An office for Arabic and national languages is charged with introducing them to school
     - Introduction of national languages is stated as an ongoing task, not a settled timetable

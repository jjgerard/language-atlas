POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1991
   description: "Loi n° 91-05 du 16 janvier 1991 portant généralisation de l'utilisation de la langue arabe, recited in the visas of the 2008 education orientation law"
 - year: 1995
   description: "Teaching of Tamazight begins in Algerian schools, in a few schools across 16 wilayas, after the year-long 'grève du cartable' school boycott in Kabylie (TSA, 2017)"
 - year: 2008
   description: "Loi n° 08-04 du 23 janvier 2008 portant loi d'orientation sur l'éducation nationale: article 33 makes Arabic the language of instruction at all levels in public and private schools, and article 34 introduces teaching of Tamazight 'pour répondre à la demande exprimée sur le territoire national'"
 - year: 2015
   description: "Minister of National Education announces extension of Tamazight teaching from 11 wilayas to 20 for the following school year (El Watan, 16 July 2015, reprinted by the HCA)"
 - year: 2020
   description: "Constitutional revision published in Journal Officiel n° 82 of 30 December 2020: article 4 states Tamazight is also a national and official language, creates an Algerian Academy of the Tamazight Language attached to the Presidency, and article 223 bars any constitutional revision touching that status"
 - year: 2021
   description: "Haut-Commissariat à l'Amazighité draws up a memorandum planning generalisation of Tamazight teaching over the period 2022-2038, aimed at ending its optional character (Liberté)"

NOTES:
 - The loi 08-04 text used is the UNESCO edurights PDF; it reproduces the JORADP French text and carries the full title "Loi n° 08-04 du 15 Moharram 1429 correspondant au 23 janvier 2008 portant loi d'orientation sur l'éducation nationale". I did not locate the JORADP original for this law in this session.
 - Article 35 of loi 08-04 handles "l'enseignement des langues étrangères" separately from articles 33 and 34, so the law's own categories are: Arabic = language of instruction, Tamazight = a taught language introduced on demand, foreign languages = a third category.
 - UNESCO PEER's Algeria inclusion profile has no "Language of instruction" heading; its only language sentence is the one quoted above, under "Ethnicity and languages". The `~curriculum` tab 404s for Algeria.

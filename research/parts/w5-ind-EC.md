NOTE ON STRUCTURE (read, and reported specifically): medium of instruction in the
nationality's language is an attribute of the SEIB, a parallel sub-system delivered through
Centros Educativos Comunitarios Interculturales Bilingues, not of every school in Ecuador.
The duty that reaches the general national curriculum is Constitution art. 347.10, and it is
framed as progressive inclusion ("de manera progresiva") of at least one ancestral language,
not as an immediate pupil entitlement.

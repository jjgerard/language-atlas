DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Regional, not national: each state picks the primary medium, so grades differ by region
     - Education and Training Policy: primary education shall be provided in the national languages
     - Since 1994 many Ethiopian languages replaced Amharic as medium where it is not the L1
     - Regional education bureaux prepare primary curricula and textbooks in those languages
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Policy frames the right as learning in the language, not learning it as a subject
     - Proclamation 41/1993 puts curricula, textbooks and minority provision with regional bureaux
     - "Not established: which languages are timetabled subjects, in which years, or on what terms"
POLICY HISTORY:

NOTE: France signed the European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages at Budapest on 7 May
1999 but has never ratified it, so there is no Committee of Experts monitoring report on France
and none is cited here. The reason is recorded in décision 99-412 DC, quoted below.

POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1999
   description: "France signs the European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages at Budapest on 7 May 1999; on 15 June 1999 the Conseil constitutionnel holds in decision 99-412 DC that the Charter contains clauses contrary to the Constitution, and France has not ratified it"
 - year: 2008
   description: "Constitutional law 2008-724 of 23 July 2008 inserts article 75-1 into the Constitution: regional languages belong to the heritage of France"
 - year: 2001
   description: "Circulaires 2001-166 and 2001-167 of 5 September 2001 set the framework for regional languages in school and for bilingual teaching at parite horaire"
 - year: 2019
   description: "Article 34 of loi 2019-791 of 26 July 2019 pour une Ecole de la confiance amends article L442-5-1 on funding pupils schooled in private contract schools teaching a regional language"
 - year: 2021
   description: "Loi 2021-641 of 21 May 2021 inserts article L312-11-2, making the regional language a subject taught within normal school hours; the same day decision 2021-818 DC strikes down its article 4 on immersive teaching and article 9 on diacritics"
 - year: 2021
   description: "Circulaire of 14 December 2021 (NOR MENE2136384C) restates the framework for regional languages and lists the languages to which it applies"

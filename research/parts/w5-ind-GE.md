NOT-ESTABLISHED NOTE:
 - No entitlement to study a minority language as a timetabled subject inside a Georgian-medium school was established from the sources consulted. Article 4 of the Law on General Education makes only the official language(s) mandatory as a subject; it says nothing about a minority language as a subject. Searched: the full consolidated English text of the Law on General Education, and the UNESCO PEER Georgia inclusion profile.
DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Whole-school provision: the vehicle is a non-Georgian-medium school, not a lesson-level right
     - Law on General Education 2005 Art 4(1): medium is Georgian, in Abkhazia Georgian or Abkhazian
     - Art 4(3): citizens whose native language is not Georgian may be schooled fully in it
     - Right runs "in accordance with the National Curriculum, as provided for by the legislation"
     - Since 2010 Azeri and Armenian entrants reach higher education via quota plus a Georgian year
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - The mandatory subject runs the other way: Georgian must be taught in minority-medium schools
     - Art 4(3): teaching of the official language is mandatory in such institutions
     - In Abkhazia, whichever official language is the medium, the other must also be taught
     - Teach Georgian as a Second Language programme targets minority-populated regions

 - The Education Strategic Plan's own note that the "Basic education curriculum has not been reviewed since 2007 (apart from maths, science, English and Ghanaian languages, which were reviewed in 2012)" is quoted above but no 2007 or 2012 instrument was retrieved, so neither is proposed as a row.

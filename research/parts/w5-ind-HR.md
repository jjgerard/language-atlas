DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - "Model A is whole schooling in the language, with at least 10 hours of Croatian a week"
     - "Model B is bilingual: social sciences in the language, 9 of 23 weekly hours in lower primary"
     - "Model A or B runs for Hungarian, Italian, Serbian and Czech, not for the other languages"
     - "Ratified Art 8.1 a(iii), b(iv), c(iv), d(iv) — on request, for pupils whose families ask"
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - "Model C is one subject: language plus its literature, history, geography, music and art"
     - "Model C could run to five hours a week but in some cases is only two"
     - "Model C reaches ten languages in primary school, six in secondary; Istro-Romanian has none"
     - "Open to all pupils, minority or not, and take-up by Croatian first-language pupils is rising"

 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - UT told the Commissioner no language is declared a minority language there
     - Media of instruction offered are Bengali, Tamil and Telugu, Class I to XII
     - Bengali is the largest: 75 primary, 25 upper primary, 11 secondary schools
     - Nicobarese, Kurukh/Oraon, Munda and Kharia appear in no education table
     - Commissioner urges the UT to appreciate the tribal languages of the Islands
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - No information furnished on any minority language taught as a subject, any stage
     - Three Language Formula names the first language simply as 'Mother tongue'
     - Third-language choices offered are Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu or Bengali
     - Commissioner notes schools and teachers for Bengali, Tamil, Telugu have fallen

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Punjabi is the only minority language reported as a medium of instruction
     - Punjabi medium runs at all four stages, Class I to XII, in 99 primary schools
     - Only 192 primary pupils are in Punjabi medium, against 98,234 taking it as subject
     - No Tamil or Urdu medium: CLM asked the UT to provide facilities for both
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Punjabi taught as a subject at all four stages, Class I to XII
     - Three-Language Formula runs Hindi/Punjabi, then Punjabi/Hindi, then English
     - All 14,085 Class VIII pupils take Punjabi, Hindi and English alike
     - 169 teacher posts sanctioned for Punjabi as a subject, 152 filled

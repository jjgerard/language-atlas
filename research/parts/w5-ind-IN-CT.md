ABSENCE NOTE: the whole of Chapter 10 names only Urdu and Punjabi as minority languages
of instruction or of study. Gondi, Halbi and Kurukh, which the atlas entry already carries
for this state, do not appear anywhere in the state's returns on medium or subject.

   bullets:
     - Urdu is the only minority language used as a medium: 3 schools, 180 pupils
     - No medium information provided for Upper Primary, Secondary or Higher Secondary
     - 340 Urdu-medium teacher posts sanctioned and all 340 filled, for 3 schools
     - Gondi, Halbi and Kurukh appear nowhere in the state's medium returns
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Urdu and Punjabi taught as subjects at Primary; Urdu alone at Upper Primary
     - Urdu and Punjabi return at Higher Secondary; Secondary stage reports nothing
     - Three-Language Formula slots each offer Hindi, English, Sanskrit or Urdu
     - SCERT trains teachers for Urdu as a subject but not as a medium

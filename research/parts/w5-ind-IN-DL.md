EXTRACTION METHOD — THE MIS-ROWING TRAP, CONFIRMED AND RESOLVED HERE: under
`pdftotext -layout` the Delhi Three-Language-Formula table renders the row
"Malayalam 18,274 8,785 1,948"; under `pdftotext -table` the same row reads
"Urdu 18,274 8,785 1,948". `-layout` displaces the number block from the language column
by several rows throughout this chapter. `-table` aligns one language to one row and is
used for every figure below; each was checked against `-table` and, where the two agreed,
against `-layout` as well. `-layout` also stacked the NDMC Three-Language-Formula values
so that "Urdu, Punjabi, Sanskrit" appeared as the SECOND language and the third slot came
out blank; `-table` shows them as the THIRD language. The `-table` reading is used.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Directorate of Education Primary medium: Urdu, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi
     - At Upper Primary and Secondary the medium narrows to Urdu, Punjabi and Bengali
     - At Higher Secondary Urdu alone is a medium, 20 schools and 3,623 pupils
     - All four municipal corporations run Urdu medium and no other language
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Twelve languages sit in the third-language slot, Arabic and Persian among them
     - Ten taught as subjects at Primary, from Gujarati and Sindhi to Tamil and Urdu
     - Urdu and Punjabi carry almost all uptake: 18,274 and 8,548 pupils in Class VIII
     - At Higher Secondary six of the twelve show zero schools and zero students

DRAFT BULLETS:

   bullets:
     - State furnished no data on facilities at Primary to Higher Secondary stages
     - Teacher posts do exist for minority-language medium: Punjabi 1,182, Urdu 48
     - Only 2 of the 48 sanctioned Urdu-medium posts are filled, against 849 of 1,182
     - No arrangement exists in the state for training minority-language teachers
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - State furnished no data on facilities at Primary to Higher Secondary stages
     - Three-Language Formula runs Hindi, then Punjabi, then English
     - Primary offices must register pupils opting for Urdu or Punjabi as extra language
     - No minority-language textbooks at Primary; Punjabi IX-X from the state board

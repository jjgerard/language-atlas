DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   value: |
     Not established from the sources consulted. The Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities
     asked Jharkhand the same medium-of-instruction question in its 48th, 50th, 51st and 52nd
     Reports (2010-11 to 2014-15); the state furnished no educational-facilities information in
     2010-11 or 2013-14 and made no reply at all in 2012-13 or 2014-15, so no medium of
     instruction is on record. This is despite Kurukh/Oraon, Santali, Mundari and Ho each being
     recorded by the state at 60 per cent or more of the population of whole districts. The
     Jharkhand Education Project Council and the state school-education portal
     (jepc.jharkhand.gov.in, schooleducation.jharkhand.gov.in) were also tried and refused
     connections from this network.
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   value: |
     Not established from the sources consulted. Jharkhand furnished no Three Language Formula
     information to either the 48th or the 51st Report of the Commissioner for Linguistic
     Minorities and did not reply to the 50th or 52nd, so no timetabled subject entitlement for
     any language is on record. The only tribal-language examination entitlement recorded in
     those four reports is outside school: a paper in Santhali, Mundari, Oraon or Ho in the
     departmental confirmation examination for State Administrative Service officers.

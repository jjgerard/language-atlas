DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Karnataka's own return to the Linguistic Minorities Commissioner, 2014-15
     - Minority-language media are Urdu, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu, Class I to X
     - At secondary stage Urdu runs 535 schools and Marathi 275, Telugu 17, Tamil 9
     - Nothing furnished on any minority-language medium at Class XI to XII
     - Tulu, Konkani and Coorgi are profiled by taluk but given no medium at all
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Urdu taught as a subject at all four stages, in 27 to 166 schools per stage
     - Tamil and Telugu taught as subjects from upper primary; Marathi not at all
     - Teacher-training institutes exist for Urdu, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil only
     - Formula's first language: Kannada, Urdu, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil or English
POLICY HISTORY:
 - year: 2016
   description: 52nd Report of the Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities records Karnataka minority-language media of instruction in Urdu, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu to Class X, with Tulu, Konkani and Kodagu given none
 - year: 2014
   description: Karnataka Government Order ED.27.Mahithi, 2012, dated 18-06-2014, constitutes the committee for recognising linguistic minority educational institutions and declares 5 Tulu, 2 Telugu, 2 Tamil and 2 Konkani institutions as linguistic-minority institutions
 - year: 1983
   description: Karnataka Education Act 1983 governs the sanction of grants-in-aid to linguistic minority educational institutions in the state

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - State's own return to the Linguistic Minorities Commissioner, 2014-15
     - Tamil and Kannada are media of instruction from Class I through Class X
     - Primary: Tamil 109 schools, Kannada 91; secondary Tamil 61, Kannada 49
     - Class XI-XII is English medium, with exams writable in Malayalam, Tamil or Kannada
     - Tulu, 18 per cent of Kasaragod Taluk, and Konkani get no medium provision
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - At Classes I-X the subjects returned are Sanskrit, Arabic and Urdu, not Tamil or Kannada
     - Tamil and Kannada are subjects only at Class XI-XII: 26 and 33 schools
     - Subject-teacher posts: Tamil 80 sanctioned and filled, Kannada none sanctioned
     - Three Language Formula is Malayalam first, English second, Hindi third

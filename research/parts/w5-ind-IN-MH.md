DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - 51st Report data: the state made no return at all to the 52nd Report
     - Media are Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu
     - Primary school counts: Urdu 1,445, Hindi 439, Kannada 140, Gujarati 91
     - All eight continue as media to Class X, Urdu running 635 secondary schools
     - At Class XI-XII only Hindi, Kannada, Sindhi and Urdu remain as media
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - The state furnished nothing at all on subject teaching, at any of the four stages
     - No information on minority languages as a subject at primary or upper primary
     - No information at secondary or higher secondary stage either
     - Three Language Formula is Marathi first, Hindi second, English third
     - No information furnished on minority-language teacher posts or training

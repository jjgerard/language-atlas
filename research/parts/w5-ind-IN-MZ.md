DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - The report's 'minority languages' for Mizoram are Bengali and Nepali, not tribal ones
     - Bengali medium in 54 primary and 17 upper-primary schools, Nepali in 10 and 3
     - At secondary stage Bengali medium schools returned Nil, Nepali returned 2
     - Mizo is an official language of the state and its own first language in school
     - Lakher, Pawi, Tripuri, Paite and Hmar appear in no education table at all
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Only Nepali is returned as a subject, in 2 primary schools, no pupil count given
     - Nothing furnished on subjects at upper primary or higher secondary stage
     - Three Language Formula is Mizo first, English second, Hindi third
     - No arrangement exists for training minority-language teachers, medium or subject

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - 2010-11 and 2012-13 CLM data; Odisha did not reply to the 51st or 52nd Report
     - Santhali is a medium in 100 primary schools, Classes I-III, in Ol Chiki script
     - Reason the state gives: Santhali's inclusion in the Eighth Schedule
     - Other media are Urdu madrasas, 149 primary schools, and Hindi in 4
     - Kui, largest tribal language of the state, is in no medium table at all
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Santhali is also listed as a subject in the same 100 primary schools
     - Primary subjects also include Telugu, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Assamese, Kannada
     - Upper primary subjects narrow to Telugu, Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu madrasa
     - Formula's first language: Oriya, Urdu, Telugu or Bengali, no tribal language

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Media at Class I-XII: Urdu, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada; secondary adds Hindi and Arabic
     - Primary media school counts: Telugu 391, Urdu 230, Kannada 47, Malayalam 29
     - Sourashtra has recognised and aided schools but is in no medium table
     - No arrangement exists for training minority-language teachers at all
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Tamil Nadu follows a TWO-language formula, not the national Three-Language Formula
     - First language is Tamil or mother tongue, second language English
     - Studying one's own language beyond that means taking an additional third language
     - Subjects returned: Urdu, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi, Gujarati, Arabic
POLICY HISTORY:
 - year: 2016
   description: 52nd Report of the Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities records that Tamil Nadu follows a Two Language Formula, Tamil or mother tongue plus English, with any other language taken as an additional third
 - year: 1998
   description: Tamil Nadu G.O. (Ms) No. 270, Higher Education (J1) Department, dated 17-06-1998, issues guidelines for conferring linguistic minority status on educational institutions
 - year: 1973
   description: Tamil Nadu Recognized Private Schools (Regulation) Act 1973, with Rules 1974, and the Tamil Nadu Minorities School (Recognition and Payment of Grants) Rules 1977, govern recognition of linguistic minority institutions
 - year: 1961
   description: Tamil Nadu G.O. (Ms) No. 455, Public (Partition) Department, dated 14.3.1961, requires government notices, rules, electoral rolls and forms to be published in minority languages in areas where 20 per cent or more speak a language other than Tamil

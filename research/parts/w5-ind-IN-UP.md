DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - State furnished no data on facilities at any stage of school education
     - U.P. Board Notification 1952 grants recognition only for education through Hindi
     - CLM says that clause deprives non-Hindi-medium schools of recognition entirely
     - Urdu is Additional Official Language, but no Urdu-medium provision is reported
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - State says no language has been declared a minority language in Uttar Pradesh
     - Three-Language Formula: Hindi first, English second, Urdu or Sanskrit third
     - Urdu may be taken as a subject at Secondary and Intermediate level
     - Board rules bar the Urdu paper from pairing with Science, Maths or Commerce
POLICY HISTORY:
 - year: 1952
   description: U.P. Board of Secondary Education Notification declares that institutions will be granted recognition only for imparting education through the medium of Hindi

 - year: 2013

NOTES ON WHAT WAS SEARCHED AND NOT FOUND:
 - The Basic Education Act No. 14 of 2013 contains only three occurrences of "language" in the whole text (s.4(f), s.4(g) and a non-discrimination clause). It sets no grade at which the medium changes and names no language.
 - kenyalaw.org returned 403 for the Act PDF under a browser user-agent; the Parliament of Kenya copy returned 200 and was used instead.
 - The UNESCO PEER Kenya ~curriculum tab returns 404; only ~inclusion exists for Kenya.
 - The BECF does not state a rule for schools whose catchment is linguistically mixed; no such passage was found on a full-text search of the framework.

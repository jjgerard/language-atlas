DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Whole schools, classes and groups operate in a minority language, not a lesson-level right
     - 2007 Education Act Art 9(3): native-language education provided "upon present of possibility"
     - Same clause makes state-language schools, classes and groups the "priority orientation"
     - UNESCO: system provides instruction in Uzbek, Uighur and Tajik alongside Kazakh and Russian
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Art 9(2): every school must give pupils knowledge of Kazakh and learning of Russian
     - State language and Russian are compulsory subjects in the final secondary assessment
     - Minority-language school-leavers therefore sit Kazakh and Russian regardless of medium
     - Kazakh-medium first-graders needing language support get an approved adaptation programme

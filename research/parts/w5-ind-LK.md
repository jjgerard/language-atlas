DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Constitution ch IV s.21(1): entitled to be educated through either National Language
     - The National Languages are Sinhala and Tamil; English is the link language (s.18-19)
     - NEC 2024: medium at primary and secondary stages is the mother tongue in practice
     - Entitlement does not reach higher education taught in a non-national language
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Mother tongue is taught as a subject from Grade 1 through Grade 11
     - Second National Language: Tamil for Sinhala pupils, Sinhala for Tamil pupils
     - Phased in from 1999 (Grades 6-9), 2001 (Grades 10-11), 2003 (Grades 3-5)
     - Oral Second National Language teaching began in Grades 1 and 2 in 2007

NOTES ON ABSENCE:
 - Article 30 of the Law on Education, read in full in the consolidated version in
   force to 31 August 2026, contains NO minimum-pupil threshold for opening
   minority-language teaching. The gate is the school's statutes plus parental and
   pupil demand.
 - The Article names no languages. Which languages are actually available is a
   matter of practice, documented only in the Advisory Committee Opinion.
 - Vocational education: the Fifth Opinion states minority languages are not taught
   there at all. I found no statutory exclusion — Art. 30 simply does not mention
   vocational programmes.

   description: "Amendments of 29 September 2022 repeal Section 9(2)(2) and Section 41 of the Education Law, ending minority-language educational programmes, and require private basic and secondary education to be in the official language"
 - year: 2022
   description: "The same amendments insert Section 47.3, obliging local governments to offer minority pupils a free interest-related education programme for the minority language and cultural history"

 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   quote: "Language rotation is defined as \"a pedagogical approach and a progressive pedagogical option that invests in multilingual education, with the aim of diversifying teaching of languages, in addition to the country's two official languages, by teaching certain subjects, particularly scientific and technical subjects, or part of the content or fragments of part of the material in one or more foreign languages.\""
   source: https://education-profiles.org/northern-africa-and-western-asia/morocco/~inclusion
DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Framework Act 51.17 Art 31: Arabic is adopted as the foundational language of instruction
     - Amazigh is to be increasingly used in schools, with no grade range or date fixed
     - Vision 2030 states the aim of making Amazigh a language of instruction, not that it is one
     - Art 31 language rotation moves science and technical subjects into foreign languages
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Art 31 promises gradually increasing multilingualism covering mastery of Arabic and Berber
     - Amazigh is an official language of the State and a shared asset for all Moroccans
     - Profile does not state which years take Amazigh or whether it is optional

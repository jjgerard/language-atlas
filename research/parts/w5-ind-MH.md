NOTES:
 - The MoE timetable models in the regulations allocate "Marshallese Language Arts 8:10-9:40 am 90 minutes daily" for grades 1-8 in Majuro and Ebeye schools, and 90 minutes daily "Marshallese" in Outer Island schools, alongside 90 minutes of English Language Arts.

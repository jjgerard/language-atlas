POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1962
   description: "Loi N° 62-74/AN-RM du 17/08/1962 launches a reform intended 'à décoloniser les esprits, à lier l'école à la vie' and to use the national languages as early as possible in schooling (as reported and cited by the UNICEF/QUALE 2021 study)"
 - year: 1979
   description: "Bamanankan first used experimentally as a medium of instruction in four schools in the Ségou and Koulikoro regions, following the 1978 second National Seminar on Education (UNICEF/QUALE 2021)"
 - year: 1987
   description: "Mali adopts 'la pédagogie convergente', beginning with two classes in Ségou in bamanankan and French; extended from 1994 (UNICEF/QUALE 2021)"
 - year: 1999
   description: "Loi n° 99-046 du 28 décembre 1999 portant loi d'orientation sur l'éducation, art. 10: 'L'enseignement est dispensé dans la langue officielle et dans les langues nationales', with modalities left to ministerial arrêté; art. 80 repeals loi n° 94-010 du 24 mars 1994 portant réorganisation de l'Enseignement"
 - year: 2005
   description: "Generalisation of the bilingual curriculum of the enseignement fondamental initiated, using eleven national languages as vehicles of instruction: bamanankan, bomu, bozo, dogosso, fulfuldé, khassonké, mamara, soninké, songhay, syenara, tamasheq (UNICEF/QUALE 2021)"
 - year: 2019
   description: "PRODEC 2 (2019-2028), dated 12 March 2019, makes phase I the progressive implantation of the langue nationale/français bilingual curriculum in the bamanan, fulfulde, songhay and tamasheq linguistic areas, and plans to introduce national languages as taught subjects in teacher-training schools"

NOTE ON DATES:
 - DATE DISCREPANCY, recorded not resolved: the law's own text reads "Loi 99 – 046 du 28 décembre 1999" and closes "Bamako, le 28 décembre 1999". The UNICEF/QUALE 2021 study cites it twice as "la loi N° 99-046/du 26 décembre 1999". The 28 December date is taken from the instrument itself.
 - The law text also records "L'Assemblée Nationale a délibéré et adopté en sa séance du 16 décembre 1999".
 - Loi 62-74 of 1962, the 1978 seminar, the 1987 pédagogie convergente and the 2005 generalisation are dated by the UNICEF/QUALE 2021 study, which is a secondary source; none of those instruments was retrieved in this session.

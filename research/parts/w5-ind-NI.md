STRUCTURE OF THE FINDING: the entitlement is territorial. Constitution art. 121 grants the
right to mother-tongue intercultural education to Caribbean Coast peoples "en su región";
outside the two Autonomous Regions the general curriculum requirement is art. 70 of Ley 582,
which puts UNITS ABOUT those languages inside the national-language (Spanish) course. That is
teaching about the languages, not teaching them.

DRAFT BULLETS:

   bullets:
     - The right is territorial: Constitution art 121 says "en su region", the two Autonomous Regions
     - Art 121: mother-tongue intercultural education, with Spanish studied as the national language
     - Ley 28 art 11.5: education in the mother tongue and in Spanish, within the national system
     - Ley 582 art 40: the regions may direct and regulate education at all levels themselves
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Outside the regions the duty is units ABOUT the languages, not teaching of them
     - Ley 582 art 70: national-language courses must carry units on Caribbean Coast languages
     - That applies to both primary and secondary programmes
     - Art 75 adds culture, customs, traditions and language of those communities to study plans

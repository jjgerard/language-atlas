CONTRADICTION NOTE:
 - The Education Act definition of "elementary education" ("in a language spoken by the student", added by No. 13 of 1995) was still in the consolidated text on PacLII when read in this session, although the 2012 announcement made English the elementary medium from 2013. The two sources are given above; the statutory text has not been shown to be repealed.

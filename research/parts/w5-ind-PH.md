SEQUENCE NOTE:
 - The subject was withdrawn before the medium was. DepEd Order 010, s. 2024 (quoted in the DepEd FAQ of 30 July 2024) removed Mother Tongue as a Grade 1-3 learning area while expressly keeping it as the medium of teaching and learning in K-3; RA 12027 then lapsed into law on 10 October 2024 and discontinued it as the medium; DepEd Order 020, s. 2025 set Filipino and English as the primary media from SY 2025-2026.

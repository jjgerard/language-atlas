ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): Ley 1264 General de Educación devotes a whole
chapter to "EDUCACIÓN PARA GRUPOS ÉTNICOS" (arts. 77-78) but that chapter contains no
language provision at all — it says only that such education follows the general principles
of the law and aims to strengthen identity and integration into Paraguayan society. The
non-Guaraní indigenous languages therefore rest on Ley 4251 art. 26 (initial stage) rather
than on the education act. Constitution art. 77 second paragraph offers ethnic minorities
whose mother tongue is not Guaraní a choice between the two OFFICIAL languages, not a right
to their own; Ley 4251 art. 26 is the provision that supplies the latter, and only for the
initial stage.

DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Guarani is a co-official majority language, not a protected minority one
     - Constitution art 77: early schooling is in the learner's official mother tongue
     - Ley 4251 art 26: indigenous peoples use their own languages at the initial stage only
     - Ley 1264 art 31: the other official language starts at once, treated as a second language
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - Ley 4251 art 28: both official languages are taught in all public and private schools
     - Art 9.6 gives a right to formal education in a mother tongue that is an indigenous language
     - Art 10.1 promises differentiated bilingual plans for indigenous peoples
     - Art 27: the school community takes part in choosing the initial literacy language

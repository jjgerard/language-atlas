DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - "Three ratification tiers: Hungarian at Art 8.1 a(i)-d(i), education IN it at every level"
     - "Rusyn and Ukrainian at level (ii) — a substantial part, read as 50% of weekly hours"
     - "Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, German, Polish, Romani only at level (iii), teaching OF"
     - "Rusyn falls short in practice: below half the time in primary, none in secondary"
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - "German is five to six lessons a week in grades 1-4, only in Chmelnica/Hopgarten"
     - "Romani a three-hour subject in four private primaries, one secondary, three vocational"
     - "Rusyn taught at least two lessons a week in three named primary schools"
     - "Some municipalities gate provision on minimum pupil numbers, which the CoE rejects"

POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 2001
   description: "Slovak Republic deposits its instrument of ratification of the European Charter on 5 September 2001, naming nine regional or minority languages and setting three different levels of Article 8 undertaking: (i) for Hungarian, (ii) for Ruthenian and Ukrainian, (iii) for Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, German, Polish and Romani"
 - year: 2002
   description: "European Charter for Regional or Minority Languages enters into force in the Slovak Republic on 1 January 2002"

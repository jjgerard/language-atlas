POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1991
   description: "Loi n° 91-22 du 16 février 1991 d'orientation de l'éducation nationale, adopted by the National Assembly on Wednesday 30 January; art. 6 makes national education 'sénégalaise et africaine', 'développant l'enseignement des langues nationales', but the law fixes no language of instruction"
 - year: 2004
   description: "Loi n° 2004-37 du 15 décembre 2004, adopted 3 December 2004, amends Loi 91-22 by inserting art. 3 bis making schooling compulsory and free for all children aged 6 to 16; it makes no change to the language provisions"
 - year: 2002
   description: "From the 2002/2003 school year Senegal opens 155 CI classes every two years, reaching 465 classes, to trial bilingual national-language/French teaching in the elementary school in six national languages: wolof, pulaar, seereer, joola, mandinka, sooninke (IFEF-OIF)"
 - year: 2023
   description: "The Modèle harmonisé d'enseignement bilingue au Sénégal (MOHEBS) enters its operational phase at the start of the 2023 school year in nine regions — Diourbel, Fatick, Kaffrine, Kaolack, Louga, Matam, Saint-Louis, Tambacounda, Kédougou — in six languages (IFEF-OIF)"

NOTE ON DATES:
 - DATE DISCREPANCY, recorded not resolved: UNESCO PEER calls it "The National Education Framework Act No. 91-22 of 30 January 1991". The instrument itself is headed "LOI N° 91.22 DU 16/2/91" and records that "L'Assemblée Nationale a délibéré et adopté en sa séance du Mercredi 30 Janvier"; the amending Loi 2004-37 also refers to it as "la loi d'orientation de l'Education Nationale n° 91-22 du 16 février 1991". 16 February 1991 is used above.
 - The hosted copy of Loi 91-22 misprints the year of the National Assembly session as "1999"; the 2004 amending law's cross-reference confirms the law is of 1991.

     - Read via the UNESCO PEER summary of the 2012 Act, not the Act text itself
     - 2012 General Education Act: indigenous language of the area is medium in ECD and P1-P3
     - Early-exit transitional model, with English taking over as medium from P4
     - Constitution Art 6(2) names English the language of instruction at all levels
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - The area's indigenous language continues as a taught subject through P4 to P8
     - 2012 Act declares all indigenous languages of South Sudan to be national languages
     - Ministry must set standards, train teachers and make materials for these languages
     - Arabic and other international languages are the optional subjects, not these

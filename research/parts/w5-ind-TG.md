POLICY HISTORY CANDIDATES:
 - year: 1968
   description: "Décret n° 68-195 du 11-11-68 portant création du Comité national d'alphabétisation"
 - year: 1975
   description: "Ordonnance n° 75-016 du 6 mai 1975 sur la réforme de l'enseignement: article 7 requires school programmes to take account of national concerns by introducing new subjects, in particular national and African languages; the reform plan set Ewe as first language in the south and (after tem was replaced by kabyè) Kabiye as first language in the north, with French kept as medium in a first stage and instruction to move into the two Togolese languages in a second stage"
 - year: 1978
   description: "Ewe and Kabiye appear in Togolese schools, but most of the 1975 reform is never applied (Leclerc); the December 2024 IOSR study dates the introduction of Kabiye and Ewe as 'langues de transmission du savoir' to 1977"
 - year: 1984
   description: "Arrêté n° 141 METQD-RS MEFDD du 30 mars 1984 portant création d'un Comité de coordination des activités sur les langues nationales (CCALN)"
 - year: 1988
   description: "Arrêté n° 27/MEN-RS du 19 février 1988 portant création d'une Commission de production de manuels scolaires within the Ministry of National Education and Scientific Research"
 - year: 2014
   description: "Education Sector Plan 2014-2025 provides a framework for a study on teaching in the national languages and for standardising three mother tongues with three textbooks per language"
 - year: 2021
   description: "Académie Kabiyè holds a validation workshop in Kara, 13-15 December 2021, adopting the kabiyè syllabaire and the primary and secondary kabiyè teaching programmes"
 - year: 2022
   description: "Togo joins the Commonwealth in June 2022 and English begins to be introduced progressively into basic education (IOSR 2024)"

NOTES:
 - I did not locate the text of Ordonnance n° 75-016 itself in this session; article 7 is quoted here as Leclerc reproduces it. I also found no Togolese loi d'orientation de l'éducation nationale of 2023 or 2024 in this session — Leclerc states that the 1975 ordonnance is the text 'qui régit l'enseignement au Togo'. Any claim that a 2024 orientation law exists should not be made on the strength of this work.
 - Leclerc gives two different numbers for the 1984 CCALN instrument on the same page ("l'arrêté de 1984 no 14/METQD-RS/MEPD" in one sentence, "arrêté n° 141 METQD-RS MEFDD du 30 mars 1984" where he reproduces its articles). The dated form is used above.
 - The 60%-of-primary-schools and one-third-of-collèges figures on the axl page come from a DIFOP fascicule circulated in 1991 and should not be presented as current.
 - The `~curriculum` tab 404s for Togo on education-profiles.org; the Togo inclusion profile has no "Language of instruction" heading, only the "Ethnicity and languages" paragraph quoted above, which is entirely about the sector plan and says nothing about which language school teaches in.

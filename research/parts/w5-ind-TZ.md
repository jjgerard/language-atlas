NOTES ON ABSENCE AND ON WHAT WAS SEARCHED:
 - The 88-page 2023 edition was read in full-text search for "lugha za asili", "lugha ya mama", "lugha za jamii" and "lugha za makabila" (indigenous / mother / community / ethnic-group languages). ZERO occurrences. The word "kabila" (ethnic group) appears only twice, both in non-discrimination clauses. The policy therefore makes no provision at all — neither as medium nor as subject — for any Tanzanian language other than Kiswahili and Tanzanian Sign Language.
 - The policy's own category for German/French/Chinese/Arabic is "lugha za kigeni" (foreign languages). English is NOT placed in that category: it is one of the two "lugha za kufundishia" (languages of instruction). This distinction is the source's own and is preserved above.
 - The original 2014 edition of the Education and Training Policy could not be retrieved. planipolis.iiep.unesco.org returned HTTP 200 but served a 3 KB HTML page, not the PDF; maelezo.go.tz and nactvet.go.tz copies 404. No claim about the 2014 text is made here except what the 2023 edition itself says about it.
 - The 2015 decision reported by UNESCO PEER (extending Kiswahili to secondary) sits awkwardly with 3.10.2 of the 2023 edition, which sets English from Form I with a permission route back to Kiswahili. Both are recorded; neither is resolved here.

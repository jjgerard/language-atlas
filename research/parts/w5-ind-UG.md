NOTES ON WHAT WAS SEARCHED AND NOT FOUND:
 - The Education (Pre-Primary, Primary and Post-Primary) Act 2008 was read in full-text search for "language" and "medium of instruction". There are exactly three hits: s.6(1)(h), s.6(1)(i), and a clause requiring School Management Committee minutes to be kept "in the local language or a language generally understood in that area". The Act sets NO medium-of-instruction rule of its own, names no grade, and names no language.
 - The Constitution's Article 6(3) is permissive, not an entitlement: another language "may be used" as a medium "as Parliament may by law prescribe". No prescribing Act was located in this session.
 - The curriculum documents state the P1-P3 rule as a recommendation carried over from the 1992 White Paper. The White Paper itself was not retrieved; no claim about its wording is made here beyond the curriculum's characterisation of it.
 - The UNESCO PEER Uganda ~curriculum tab returns 404.
 - The curriculum does not name which local languages are approved as media, nor how a school's language is chosen. That determination was not established from the sources consulted.

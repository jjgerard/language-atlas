NOTE ON MODAL VERBS (read, and reported specifically): the constitutional duty in art. X §4 is
"shall" — the State SHALL provide a Hawaiian education program of language, culture and
history in the public schools. Chapter 302H, by contrast, is permissive throughout: the
programme "may be established" (§302H-1), the department "may create" an office (§302H-3),
the superintendent "may provide" facilities or transportation once fifteen children in a
district wish to enrol (§302H-4), and the department "may establish" the statewide agency
(§302H-6). The two "shall" clauses in chapter 302H bind the agency's scope (§302H-6) and the
duty to collaborate with UH Hilo (§302H-7), not the duty to open a school.

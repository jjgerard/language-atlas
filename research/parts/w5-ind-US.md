ABSENCE (read, and reported specifically): NALA §104 is a DECLARATION OF POLICY. Every clause
bearing on schools uses "encourage", "support", "allow" or "recognize"; none creates a pupil
entitlement to be taught in or taught a Native American language in a state public school.
The one clause that speaks of a right (§104(5)) is limited to schools funded by the Secretary
of the Interior. §105 is a non-restriction rule, not a provision rule. The Esther Martinez
Act works through competitive grants under the Native American Programs Act, and sets
eligibility floors (10 children under 7; 15 students; 500 hours) rather than duties on
school systems.

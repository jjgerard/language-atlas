NOTES:
 - The Schedule to the Education Act 2014 lists every government school with a single "Language" column recorded as E or F. No school in that register is recorded as vernacular or Bislama medium.
 - Section 6 makes no vernacular entitlement: the only route to vernacular teaching of a subject is a ministerial Order for named schools under s.6(5). No such Order was located in this session.

CONTRADICTION NOTE:
 UNESCO PEER states the 1997 LiEP "require[s] learners to be taught in their native language in the Foundation Phase". The LiEP text read here contains no such requirement: it vests the choice of language of learning and teaching in the individual, requires only that the language be an official one, and leaves the school's language policy to the governing body subject to the 40/35 practicability threshold. Both sources are given; the atlas should not assert the Foundation Phase requirement in its own voice.

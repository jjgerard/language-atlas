DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: indigenous.mediumOfInstruction
   bullets:
     - Duty is to endeavour, not to provide: Education Amendment Act 2019 s.62(1)
     - s.62(1)(c): mother tongue is to be used as a medium of instruction at ECD
     - s.62(1)(b) ties the language of instruction to the language of examination
     - UNESCO PEER records mother-tongue teaching running from grade 1 to grade 3
 - field: indigenous.taughtAsSubject
   bullets:
     - s.62(1)(a): every school shall endeavour to teach every officially recognised language
     - Constitution recognises 16 official languages, so the duty covers all of them
     - Ministry position is that English, Shona and Ndebele be taught in all schools equally
     - Ministry has developed minority-language syllabi and books for O and A level

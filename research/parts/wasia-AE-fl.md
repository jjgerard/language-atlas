DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Arabic is the language of instruction in public schools
     - English is taught as a second language under the MOE framework
     - KG and Cycle 1 teach maths and science primarily in Arabic
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Selective Cycle 1-3 streams also teach maths and science in English
     - English-medium subjects include technical and health science subjects
     - Private schools are free on curriculum except Arabic and social studies
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - English is the main additional language across public schooling
     - Private schools run Arabic as a second language for non-Arabic speakers
     - Common private curricula are UK, US, Indian and International Baccalaureate
 - field: assessment
   bullets:
     - English curriculum outcomes are benchmarked to the CEFR
     - EmSAT assesses core areas of language (English, Arabic), science and mathematics
 - field: policyHistory
   bullets:
     - 2017 MOE review of the KG-12 English curriculum framework
     - 2018 new national English curriculum framework implemented
     - National Literacy Strategy supports Arabic reading development

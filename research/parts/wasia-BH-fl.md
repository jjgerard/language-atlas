DRAFT BULLETS:

   bullets:
     - Arabic is the language of instruction in all public schools
     - English is a compulsory foreign language subject
     - French is offered as an elective second foreign language
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - Compulsory: English. Elective: French
     - French is delivered via the French Language Teaching Project in intermediate schools
     - Most private schools present all subjects in English, some in Arabic or French
 - field: secondaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Cycle 4 adds two reading periods a week, one Arabic and one English
     - Private schools must still use MoE curricula for Arabic, Islamic studies and civics
 - field: curriculumTime
   bullets:
     - Iqra'a project: 30-minute daily reading period across Cycles 1-3
     - Split three times a week Arabic reading, two times a week English reading
     - One optional weekly French reading slot in participating intermediate schools

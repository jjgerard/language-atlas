DRAFT BULLETS:
 - field: regionalMinorityLanguages
   bullets:
     - Category matters: these are constitutional mother tongues, not foreign languages
     - Constitution Art. 4 and 2011 Ministerial Decree No. 22 give a mother-tongue right
     - "Turkmen, Assyrian and Armenian are expected to be taught in public schools"
     - TIMSS names Kurdish, Turkmen and Syriac as constitutionally approved curriculum languages
 - field: languagesOffered
   bullets:
     - English is the foreign language; some curricula run wholly in English
     - "schools for the distinguished" teach science and maths only in English
     - Kurdish, Turkmen and Syriac are media of instruction, not foreign-language subjects
 - field: primaryRequirement
   bullets:
     - Public schools use unified national curricula issued in approved languages
     - Constitution encourages bilingual schools "that teach the two languages"
 - field: policyHistory
   bullets:
     - 1998 Law of Education sets the system's objectives
     - 2011 Ministerial Decree No. 22 on Education enshrines mother-tongue education
     - 2023 Iraq joins TIMSS for the first time
